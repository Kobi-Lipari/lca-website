## Note on the focus-ring flake after step 31 (Thu, Oct 22, morning)

The CI accessibility job failed on c52848e (step 31) in one test, on the flags-off build: components-edges.spec.ts "the gold Button shows a focus ring when the keyboard reaches it" read the ring once, right after Tab, and the read landed before the ring had its width.
