How Remind me differs here

  • No "Registration opens" or "Early price ends" options: LCA doesn't know a partner event's entry dates or fees.
  • "If this listing changes" is a reminder of kind 'listing_changed'. The morning sync compares today's feed row with yesterday's and sends one email per change, using the same words as the Listing history row.
  • When the date is not confirmed, the two date reminders are greyed out and "If this listing changes" is ticked for you (crop B3).

Where this board stops

  • LCA-run and club-run event pages are on Event-Final-1 and Event-Final-2.
  • The partner page on a phone (facts card, source line, the "Registers on the organizer's site ↗" bar) is on Event-Phones-Final.
  • How a partner row reads in the Tournaments list is on Tourn-List-Final.
