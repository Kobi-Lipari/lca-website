Notes on this board

  1. 1Who opens it. A signed-in member with an active Family membership. Everyone else signed in meets the same picker as step 1 of Register.
  2. 2Only the membership is shared. It covers you and up to 3 children. There is one entry price per section and tier, no member price; every player pays their own section's fee.
  3. 3Everyone starts unticked. Ticking Playing opens that person's selects.
  4. 4Names as the TD sees them, with the public form for a child ("Maya R." before the event). Edit name uses the existing guardian-scoped PATCH.
  5. 5Only sections this person can enter, side events included, each with its one price and its count. The hint says what is hidden and why.
  6. 6The grade box appears once a scholastic section is picked: required, one yes or no.
  7. 7The schedule select only with two schedules, and only for a main-event section.
  8. 8Add a family member: names and an optional US Chess ID, no email. The row says how many plan spots are left. Up to 9 players an order.
  9. 9Continue stays disabled until each ticked person has a section and the names are confirmed.
  10. 10The running total is entry fees only. Step 3 adds an LCA membership for anyone without one; no card fees are added at payment.
  11. 11Duplicate name, drawn once: "That name is already in your family".
  12. 12Row states: registered by the other guardian, name locked, not on the plan (a Scholastic membership is added in step 3), a tenth player.
  13. 13Signed out, the button explains first: three steps and the picker with sample names, badged.
  14. 14Three ways on, each one back to this picker: join, create a login, or Log in.
  15. 15Phone: a full-screen sheet, tab bar hidden, a 72px bar with the total and Continue. No price or count under a select and no detail line under a name: the rating sits beside the name, the public name moves into Edit name (F4).
  16. 16The select opens as a bottom sheet with the same groups, prices, counts and hint: the one place on the phone that shows them.
  17. 17The family limit is enforced. A Family plan covers 3 children: "1 spot left", then "plan is full" (a 4th child still joins with a Scholastic membership), and at 8 profiles the link is disabled.
  18. 18The sheet scrolls inside itself. The steps stay at the top and the list scrolls beneath; on phones the total and Continue bar stays at the bottom. Drawn here at full length.
