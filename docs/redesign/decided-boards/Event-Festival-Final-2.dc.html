B3Take offline asks first

Take the festival page offline?

The page stops showing at /festivals/morphy-2027 and leaves the home page. The three events stay open for registration on their own pages, and nobody's entry changes.

How the switches behave

  • Status moves Draft, then Live, then Offline; Go live again brings it back with the same address.
  • Feature on the home page is off and disabled in Draft and Offline, with the reason in words.
  • Turning it on for a second festival asks first: "This replaces the feature on [other festival]". One at a time.
  • The feature ends on its own after the end date; no one has to switch it off.

Every field in spec section 7 maps to a festivals column (name, slug, start_date, end_date, city, venue, hero_photo_url, tagline, intro, blocks_json, visit_json, partners_json, faq_json, status, featured, created_by); the picker writes festival_events with position.
