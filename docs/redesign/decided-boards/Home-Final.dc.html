  1. 1Hero, rung 5 Register. An open LCA event starts within 30 days, or its early entry ends within 14. Home-A's card, the hall photo (LCA modes only) and exactly one gold primary.
  2. 2Same card, by registration state. Full shows Join the waitlist; closed online shows Event details. "You're entered" is added in the browser from your account, never cached.
  3. 3Should I hurry? Count, spots left and the early-entry date in one line, with a fill bar. Counts appear on LCA events only.
  4. 4Also this weekend. A Louisiana partner event within 7 days gets one foot line. No Register, no count; the link opens the organizer's site in a new tab.
  5. 5Identity and search band, under every hero mode, so the box never moves. Each stat is a link. Results open below the field only on click or Tab, as an overlay that never pushes the page.
  6. 6Quick picks stay hidden until WS04 and WS09 ship the filters they point at. Drawn greyed here so the slot is known.
  7. 7This-week strip waits for WS09 (flag homeWeek). Free-text club schedules are never parsed into a Tonight list; a wrong time sends a parent to a locked door.
  8. 8Upcoming six: Louisiana only, the hero's event removed, status in words, counts on LCA rows only. All tournaments covers the rest of the Gulf South.
  9. 9Doors band replaces Home-A's "Start here" row. Three doors, three links each, each landing on a shared page named in grey.
  10. 10Results and champions. Heritage accent in Geist (thick–thin rule, seal), no heritage fonts on the homepage. Hidden when the newest title is over 13 months old. Names never link (D9). The recap card waits for WS10.
  11. 11News and a compact Facebook rail. Three dated posts; Facebook posts are fetched on the server.
  12. 12Membership band, hidden for active members. Then the decided F1 footer with the trust line and the Appearance control.
