Notes on this board

1Side by side

  1. 1One toolbar. The same toolbar as every view. Search filters the list and the pins together.
  2. 2Distance is optional. A typed city or ZIP only, in straight-line miles. Nothing is saved, no drive times, no location prompt.
  3. 3One view. List and map move together: hovering a row lifts its pin, clicking a pin selects and scrolls its row.
  4. 4The card is B’s preview made small. Facts, count, early price. Register appears only on LCA events.
  5. 5Bell. On hover it replaces Tourn-D’s alert card and any calendar subscription. A set reminder stays visible as On.
  6. 6Regions. A region button sets the Region chip and the link. Empty regions point to the clubs that meet there.
  7. 7Pins never fade. Clusters show their count; the hovered row’s pin gets a navy ring.

2Map first, list drawer

  1. 1Map takes the width. The list floats in a drawer that can hide with one button.
  2. 2Two counts, two jobs. The drawer counts what is in view; the toolbar count stays the filter total.
  3. 3Clusters list in place. Clicking a cluster lists its events right there, no zoom hunting.
  4. 4Select, then Back. Selecting swaps the drawer for the preview; Back returns to the same scroll position.
  5. 5Signed-out bell. Asks to log in or create an account, then comes back here with the reminder ready.
  6. 6Partner exit. The partner preview has no count and no Register; gold is never used for a partner exit.
  7. 7Region pills. They set the Region chip and the link, like the chips do.

3Regions first

  1. 1The regions are the map. The seven official regions, with the name and number always beside the shade.
  2. 2Edge tabs. Neighbouring states are tabs with counts; Gulf South pins are one click away.
  3. 3Grouped list. Grouped by region, soonest first inside each; a Month grouping is one menu away.
  4. 4Hover and click. Hovering a region highlights its group; clicking sets the Region chip and the link.
  5. 5A chosen region. Shows its pins and the clubs that meet there.
  6. 6Bell options fit the event. Only the dates an event has: here the early price.
  7. 7Approximate outlines. They need a parish-to-region table; regions.ts maps clubs today, not parishes.

The same in all three

  • Decided header with the rook mark. Signed in it reads Donate · My LCA (a valid member, so no Renew); signed out, Donate · Log in · Join LCA.
  • Toolbar: Upcoming 25 | Results; List · Calendar · Map · Table centred with Map on; search to its right; When · Type · Region chips; 16 tournaments, removable chips, Clear all, Copy link to this view.
  • Filled navy pins are LCA events (register here); hollow pins are partner events (organizer’s site); a numeral marks several events. Lafayette Open 2026 is a partner event.
  • Partner rows say “Registers on the organizer’s site ↗”, never Register, never a count.
  • The bell sits at the right end of every row, shown on hover or focus; one week and one day before are ticked by default.
  • No drive times, no “Use my location”, no alert card, no subscribe card, no Show clubs toggle. The map is an inline drawing, not map tiles.

For K to pick: 1, 2 or 3, or a mix (for example option 3’s region shapes as the “Louisiana only” mode of option 1 or 2).

Side by side

Tourn-D’s list beside the map, cleaned up: A-style rows with the bell, a sticky map on the right with an anchored B-style event card, optional distance rings from a typed city or ZIP, and region counts under the map.

Frame 1440 · signed in · Baton Rouge Classic selected

Best for

Comparing when and where together, for example a parent choosing between Baton Rouge and Lafayette this month. It is the closest to the Tourn-D layout K already loves.

Trade-off

Two columns need about 1200px, so the map is only 768px wide and Houston and the Jackson area still cluster at this zoom. Distance rings need a cached ZIP or city lookup, and they show straight-line miles, not driving.

Feedback applied

Rows take A’s display; the card takes B’s preview content; the bell replaces Tourn-D’s “Create alert” card; the decided toolbar; drive times, “Use my location” and the drive-time control are gone, replaced by optional straight-line distance; Lafayette Open is a partner (hollow pin, organizer exit).

Other widths and phones

Below 1200px the grid becomes one column: the map (360px tall) above the list, the card dropping under the map as a static block. On phones: “map on top, list sheet that drags up”. Signed in here because the popover shows the signed-in state.
