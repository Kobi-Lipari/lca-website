C2Month beside the list

  1. 1Rail calendar. Tap a day or a bar and the list scrolls to that week. Bars have no words; the names are in the list beside it.
  2. 2Season strip. Counts per month in one line. A month outside the When range sets When to that month.
  3. 3Legend. Calmer than Tourn-C: two thin lanes at most, no deadline ticks, no club nights, no layers. The shaded row follows your scroll.
  4. 4Signed-out bell. Signed out, the bell asks you to log in or create an account; nothing is collected first.
  5. 5Weeks start Monday, so a Sat–Sun event stays in one week section and one bar.

Month beside the list

The brief’s calendar done quietly: a small Monday-first month grid with unlabeled bars and a season strip lives in the right rail as a navigator, and the main column is a week-grouped list in Tourn-A rows.

Frame 1440 · signed out · signed-out bell popover open

Best for

People who think in month grids (“is the 24th free?”) and the brief’s acceptance criteria as written: season strip with counts, Monday-first grid, multi-day bars, today marked, tap a day to scroll the list. Lowest build risk; it reworks Tourn-C rather than replacing it.

Trade-off

The grid only navigates: with no names on the bars you read the list to know what a bar is. The rail holds the grid plus three cards, and once a row is selected the grid and the preview share 424px, so the rail gets long. Big weekends need “+1”.

Feedback applied

Calmer than Tourn-C: the grid shrinks to a rail navigator, bars lose their text, lanes go from three to two, deadline ticks and club nights leave the grid, and the gold frame becomes a soft shaded row. A’s rail sits under the grid; B’s preview takes the place of the three cards when a row is selected, with the grid staying on top.

Other widths and phones

The 424px rail leaves 54px day columns at 1440; at 1280 they drop to 48px. Below 1200px the calendar card moves above the list at full width and the cards drop below the list. Phones (later board): a collapsible month card at the top (48px week rows, 44px day targets), collapsed to the current week, with the season strip as a sideways scroller; the list follows in Tourn-Phones cards.
