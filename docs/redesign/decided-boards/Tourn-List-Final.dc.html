Notes on this board

  1. 1Toolbar, same on every view. Upcoming 25 | Results on the left, List · Calendar · Map · Table centred, the search field on its right. Search covers name, city and organizer.
  2. 2One filter and sort line, 44px. Live count on the left, then When · Type · Region, the applied chips (each removable), Clear all, and Sort at the far right. No Rated or LCA-run chip, no Copy link; filters still live in the address bar.
  3. 3LCA rows keep "LCA event", status in words, "X of Y registered" with a fill bar and the next deadline. Register is the only gold button on a row. Format in plain words, G/90+30 in the glossary.
  4. 4Rail before a selection: New to tournaments? (the first-tournament guide lives here now), Latest results, For organizers. No subscribe card anywhere.
  5. 5Partner rows are lighter and say "Registers on the organizer's site ↗" (opens a new tab). Never Register, never a count. Lafayette Open 2026 is a partner event.
  6. 6Month headings carry counts and pin under the filter line while scrolling. "This weekend" gets its own sub-heading.
  7. 7Selecting a row swaps the rail for the preview. Both are 400px, so the list never reflows. The selected row gets a cream fill and a gold ring.
  8. 8The address holds the selection: event= is added to the URL in the address bar, so a shared address opens this exact preview. No link strip or Copy link in the pane.
  9. 9Live per-section counts come from registrations and add up to the total (4 + 6 + 8 + 5 = 23). "Places left" in words. One entry price per section (Section · Entry · Registered): there is no member price, since an LCA membership is required to enter and is added at checkout.
  10. 10Keyboard: J and K move through rows, Enter opens the full page, Esc closes the preview and returns the rail.
  11. 11Results tab: State champions first (one line per title, honor roll link), then LCA events by month with Standings and Crosstable. Champion names are plain text, never links.
  12. 12Bell, on hover or keyboard focus, in its own 44px column at the row's right edge, so nothing shifts. Opens "Remind me": registration opens, early price, one week, one day (the last two ticked). Options that no longer apply show why.
  13. 13Info note under the filter line. One muted card with an info icon: the date range, how many register on LCA and how many are partner listings, and where each kind registers. Nothing else sits between the filters and the list.
  14. 14Who's registered, in the preview. Players under 18 show as first name and last initial (Priya S., Theo G.); adults in full. Directors and admins see full names. Results, standings and winners keep full names (decision D3 unchanged).

How the pane behaves

  • Opens on click, Enter or J/K. Esc or Close brings the rail back where it was.
  • Sticky beside the list from 1280px. Below that a row opens the full event page; on phones it is a sheet rising to about 85% height, with "‹ Back to list" at its top left (decided phone board).
  • Partner panes keep the clearinghouse facts and one navy exit to the organizer, plus the same Remind me, Add to calendar, Share and Full event page buttons. No count, no fee table, no Register.
  • "Remind me" in a pane opens the same popover as the row's bell.
  • Not drawn here: full (waitlist) and finished states; they reuse the hero card wording from Home.
