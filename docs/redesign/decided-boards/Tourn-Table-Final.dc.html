  3. 3No bell column. The table starts at Date. Date and Event stay frozen when the table scrolls sideways; the column headers sort too and stay in step with Sort.

  8. 8Keyboard hint moved to the table footer: J and K move, Enter opens the full page, Esc clears. The CSV download link is gone.
