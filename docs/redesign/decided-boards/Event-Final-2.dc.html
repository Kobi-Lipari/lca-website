Good to know

Full rules and FAQ

Time control

G/90+30: 90 minutes each, plus 30 seconds after every move. A game can run about 4 hours.

US Chess membership

Required, and current on Sat, Jun 12. We check your US Chess ID as you enter and warn you if it lapses.

Byes

Can't make a round? Take up to 2 half-point byes in Rounds 1–4, each worth ½. Ask when you register or before Round 2.

Arriving

Check in by 9:30 AM on Sat, Jun 12. Not at your board 30 minutes after a round starts? That game is a forfeit.

Withdrawing

Full refund until Wed, Jun 9. After that, your fee becomes credit toward another LCA event.

Membership

An LCA membership is required to enter and is added at checkout if you need one. Family plans cover you and up to 3 children.
