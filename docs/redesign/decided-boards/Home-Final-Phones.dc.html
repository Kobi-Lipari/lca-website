Sample data

Events, clubs, cities and people are the ones the other boards use. Entry counts, fees, live times, results and the Downriver Quads row are sample. Each phone shows its own moment.

Notes on this board

  1. 1App bar. 56px navy: rook mark and "Louisiana Chess Association", tap for Home. A 44×44 bordered Search button opens the /search page, so Back works. It never condenses.
  2. 2Tab bar. Tournaments · Clubs · Log in · Menu, labels always shown, 56px plus the safe area, never hidden on scroll. The current tab gets a bold label and a 3px gold-ink bar. Home is not a tab, so none is marked here.
  3. 3Register hero at 390px. Home-A's card: 96px hall photo, two tags, 26px title, weekday dates, format in words, sections and fees, one gold primary. While loading, the hero holds 360px with a plain skeleton.
  4. 4Should I hurry? Count and spots left over a 6px bar, early entry on its own line. Counts only on LCA events. Within 14 days this is the last-call rung, the same card with "Starts Saturday · in 4 days".
  5. 5Identity and search band, under every hero mode: official logo, linked stats, the thick–thin rule and a 48px box with a visible label. Quick picks stay hidden until their filters ship.
  6. 6Phone order (WS03 AC5): the brief's order with the band added. Nothing is hidden by hero mode; flagged blocks are absent until their data exists.
  7. 7Live hero. Live colour tokens on this card only, Geist type, no live fonts on Home. One gold button. Signed-in players also get "You're on board 6 with White". The Tournaments tab carries a worded Live tag.
  8. 8Rounds in words. A visible "Rounds" heading; "Round" is in each item for screen readers, never an abbreviation. Updates every 20 seconds while the tab is visible; Pause updates stops them. Nothing moves.
  9. 9Results hero. Heritage accent in Geist: thick–thin rule and seal, no photo. One winner per section by full name with ½, ties in words, up to four sections. Names never link to a player page.
  10. 10This weekend in Louisiana. Up to three dated rows. Partner rows say where you register and open the organizer's site in a new tab: never a Register button, a count or the LCA hall photo. Ends with the Next LCA event line.
  11. 11Quiet. The usual state: the next LCA event, the three soonest Louisiana events however far, and clubs by region. Shown signed in: My LCA replaces Log in, with a count of personal to-dos.
  12. 12Clubs floor. When nothing else matches, or when the home data fails to load, the hero is built from the static region list with no error message. Lower blocks keep their own honest error states.
  13. 13Doors band as an accordion. One door open at a time, opened by a real button with aria-expanded. Three links each, with the landing page named in grey. It opens in place without animation.
