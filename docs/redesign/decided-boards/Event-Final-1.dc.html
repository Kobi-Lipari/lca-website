Good to know

Full rules and FAQ

Time control

G/90+30: 90 minutes each, plus 30 seconds after every move; a game can run about 4 hours. The 2-day schedule plays Rounds 1–3 at G/45+10.

US Chess membership

Required, and current on Sat, Sep 4. We check your US Chess ID as you enter and warn you if it lapses.

Byes

Can't make a round? Take up to 2 half-point byes, each worth ½, in Rounds 1–5. Ask when you register or before Round 2 is paired.

Arriving

3-day players check in by 9:30 AM on Sat, Sep 4; 2-day players by 7:40 AM on Sun, Sep 5. Not at your board 30 minutes after a round starts? That game is a forfeit.

Withdrawing

Full refund until Thu, Sep 2. After that, your fee becomes credit toward another LCA event.
