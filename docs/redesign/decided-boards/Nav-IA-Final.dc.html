Notes on this board
1Six sections in one row, the same order at every width. Only About opens a menu (on click, with a caret). The current section is light gold with a 2px gold underline and aria-current="page".
2One nav.ts: every destination has one entry and one label. The header, Menu sheet, tabs, footer, trust line and the search Popular list all read it, so a page is never called two things.
3Each page lives under one section. Pages reached only from a list (an event, a club, a news post) are shown in grey type; they are not menu links.
4Routes that arrive later are tagged with their workstream. Their menu, sheet, footer and search rows are gated on the same flag as the route, so no menu ever holds a dead link.
5The utility trio, in this order on every page: Donate · Log in · Join LCA. Signed in: Donate · My LCA ▾. Renew takes the Join slot within 30 days of expiry or once lapsed.
6My LCA ▾ holds to-dos first, then personal pages, then volunteer tools by role (from today’s useAccountLinks() and roles.ts), then Log out.
7Phones (under 768px): four tabs, Tournaments · Clubs · Log in or My LCA · Menu. The first tab says “Tournaments”, the same word as desktop.
8The Menu sheet holds everything the tabs do not, in a fixed order. Donate is the first button after the section list.
9Footer F1: four columns of nav.ts entries by id, F3’s trust line and the Appearance control. No Privacy or Accessibility links until those pages exist.
10Where the replan’s own lists used two words for one page, this board picks one. Section F lists each choice; test/unit/nav.test.ts fails on any second label.
