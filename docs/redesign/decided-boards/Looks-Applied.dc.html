Notes on this board

  1. 1One rule an engineer can apply without asking. A page with a form, a filter, a queue, a deadline or a payment is Minimal. Official, permanent or ceremonial content that changes a few times a year is Heritage.
  2. 2Every route, by scope. Each chip names its scope in words; the colour only repeats it. A unit test over the route-to-scope map keeps workspace and admin out of Heritage.
  3. 3Header and footer are always the base look, on every page and in every scope, including Heritage and Broadcast.
  4. 4Controls never change. Inside Heritage, Button, Input, chips, StatusBadge words and colours, the focus ring and the control radius are the base components. Only type, rules, the seal and grounds change.
  5. 5Editors and forms reset to base through ThemeScope scope="base" (data-theme=""), the one permitted nesting. Heritage never renders inside live.
  6. 6Minimal is for doing. Lists, filters, Register and checkout. Partner events never get Register or a count; they register on the organizer's site.
  7. 7Broadcast while a round is live: pairings, standings, My board, hall TV and the event page. The Live dot is static; the word "Live" carries the meaning.
  8. 8Heritage Level A, full scope: six routes today, keepsakes later, and the HonorBoard above final standings of a completed state championship.
  9. 9Heritage Level B, accent only, on the homepage: thick–thin rule, official logo and seal, all in Geist. No heritage font is ever fetched on /.
  10. 10One token layer. --gold-ink resolves to #705718 inside Heritage and #866a1e in base. Under .dark, Heritage keeps its type, rules and seal on the Minimal dark grounds.
  11. 11Fonts load only inside their scope, lazily. Heritage is at most 110 KB of woff2. Geist Mono is the only mono face, in every scope.
  12. 12Bright Scholastic is kept, for /scholastic/* only, built in WS11 behind themeScholastic (off). Until then /scholastic is Minimal.

The rule

Scope rule · one sentence

Minimal is for doing, Heritage is for the lasting record, Broadcast is for what is live right now.

The one exception: Bright Scholastic stays planned for /scholastic/* only, and ships later. The header and footer are always the base look, on every page.

Base · site-wide · Looks-3

Modern Tech-Minimal

For everything a person uses or acts on: finding, filtering, registering, paying, managing.

  • Geist and Geist Mono, one Instrument Serif accent
  • Navy ink on white, gold as a fill only
  • Light and dark, toggleable

Test: does the page have a form, a filter, a queue, a deadline or a payment? Then it is Minimal.

Record · six pages · Looks-1

Heritage Club

For LCA's permanent record: who runs it, its rules, its titles, its keepsakes.

  • Caslon headlines, Source Serif 4 body
  • Thick–thin rules, the seal, small-caps labels
  • Paper grounds; controls stay base

Test: is it official, permanent or ceremonial, and does it change a few times a year? Then it is Heritage.

Live · event day · Looks-7

Broadcast Night

For what is live right now: a round is in progress at an LCA event.

  • Dark live tokens, gold accent
  • Barlow only on live routes; Geist in the header strip
  • Static Live dot, the word carries it

Test: is a round live, or has the person turned on Night? Then it is Broadcast.

Exception · later · Looks-6

Bright Scholastic

Kept for /scholastic/* only. Not decided by K, so planned and flagged off, never silently dropped.

  • Built in WS11 behind themeScholastic
  • WS01 ships only its tokens and an axe fixture
  • Until then /scholastic is Minimal

Never on registration, checkout, shared event pages, pairings or standings.

Rules every scope keeps

  • Status is words plus colour, never colour alone.
  • Brand gold #c8a94a is never text on a light ground. It is a fill with navy text, a rule, a seal, or text on navy and dark grounds.
  • Gold-toned text on light grounds uses --gold-ink.
  • Field borders stay at 3:1 or more against their ground.
  • The 2px navy focus ring is the same everywhere (gold on live).
  • Scopes nest one level only. Base inside Heritage is the one permitted nesting. Heritage never renders inside live.
  • Nothing animates. The live dot is static.
