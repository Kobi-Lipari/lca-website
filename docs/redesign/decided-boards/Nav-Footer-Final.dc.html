Notes on this board
1Brand block. The official logo on the same 44px cream plate as the header (D1: never recoloured, set on cream), the name, one sentence, then Join LCA (gold fill, navy text) and Donate. It never folds, at any width.
2Four columns of nav.ts entries by id. One label per destination, the same words as the header, Menu sheet and search: "Board & regions", "Bylaws & rules" and "Minutes" (two links, never merged), "State champions", "Contact".
3Rows that arrive later. Calendar (WS04) and Results (WS10) are drawn dashed. In the build each renders only when its route's flag is on, so the footer never holds a dead link.
4A real inbox first. LouisianaChess@gmail.com is a mailto link at the top of Contact (brief AC7), then the contact form, membership help, website help (/support) and Donate.
5LCA on Facebook ↗ opens in a new tab and says so to screen readers. With the About menu's footer row it keeps the Facebook link the brief requires (rule 0.1.4).
6F3's trust line. Bylaws & rules and Minutes link to their pages. "Site last updated" is written at build time, with its weekday. No Privacy or Accessibility links until those pages exist.
7Appearance: System · Light · Dark. A three-button radio group, the one theme switch at every width (the Menu sheet's switch is a shortcut to it). Stored in localStorage inside try/catch; default System. The chosen one is gold with a check, not colour alone.
8Under 768px the columns fold. Each heading becomes <h2><button aria-expanded aria-controls> in a 52px row. Contact starts open, the rest collapsed; each group opens on its own; nothing animates.
9Phone base line. The trust line stacks one fact per line; Bylaws & rules and Minutes keep 44px targets. Appearance spans the width in three 44px buttons.
10Clears the tab bar. <main>'s padding-bottom comes from --bottom-chrome-h, so at the end of the page the Appearance control sits fully above the tabs, never behind them.
11Dark mode. The footer stays navy #1a2744, as the header does; the page ground turns #0b1220 and the tab bar #111a2c. The logo plate stays cream. Gold text is fine on these grounds.

How the fold works
One Footer.tsx; the headings turn into buttons only under 768px.

8Markup under 768px

<h2>
  <button aria-expanded="true"
          aria-controls="footer-contact">
    Contact
  </button>
</h2>
<ul id="footer-contact">…</ul>
  • Starts with Contact open, Play, Community and About LCA collapsed (hidden on their lists).
  • Each group is its own switch. Opening Play leaves Contact open; it is not an accordion.
  • 52px heading rows, 44px link rows, 16px side gutter. The chevron turns; nothing slides, at any motion setting.
  • Tab order: brand link, Join LCA, Donate, then each heading button, the open lists, the trust-line links and Appearance. Every footer link is reachable by keyboard at 390px.
  • 768px and up: the buttons go away and the headings are plain <h2> over always-open lists, as in A.
