Quick-pick chips stay hidden until WS04 and WS09 ship their filters. One gold primary per hero: the band's Search button and the header's Join LCA are site chrome, the same in every mode, as on Home-Final.
  3. 10Announced: signed in gets the reminder; signed out gets Event details and a calendar file that collects nothing, never "Log in to get a reminder". No venue is stored yet, so the city line is dropped, not printed as a placeholder.
  4. 11Grid motif (a–h, 8–1, 0 KB) on partner and quiet modes. A partner event never wears LCA's hall photo.
  5. 12Partner rows: the "Partner event" tag and "Registers on the organizer's site ↗", never Register and never a count. They drop out when the feed is more than 3 days old.
  6. 13The Next LCA event line ends every non-LCA mode, status in words. It is omitted when nothing is announced within 180 days, as on the Week frame (Mon, Oct 5), and on the Clubs floor, which has no event data.
  7. 14Quiet is the usual homepage, so it carries real dated events however far away, and clubs, which meet every week and suit beginners and kids.
  8. 15Clubs floor: never blank. It is also what the client shows when /api/home fails, drawn from the static regions list with no error message.
