Notes on this board

  1. 1One filter and sort line, 44px. Live count at the left, When · Type · Region, the applied chips (each removable), Clear all, then Sort at the far right. Same line on every view. No Copy link; the address still holds the filters.
  2. 2Info note under the line. One muted card with an info icon: the date range, how many register on LCA, how many are partner listings, and where each kind registers. Nothing else sits between the filters and the map.
  3. 3Map takes the width. The list floats in a 400px drawer on the left and hides with one button. Between 1024 and 1279px the drawer narrows to 340px.
  4. 4Two counts, two jobs. The filter line counts the filter total; the drawer counts what is in view. The drawer lost its own Sort button: Sort lives once, at the end of the filter line, and orders the drawer.
  5. 5Clusters list in place. Clicking a numbered pin lists its events right there, no zoom hunting.
  6. 6Bell on hover. Opens Remind me with one week and one day ticked. Partner events can’t remind about registration or price dates, and say why. Signed out, the bell asks to log in (frame D).
  7. 7Region pills set the Region filter and its chip, like the dropdown does. Empty regions point to their weekly clubs.
  8. 8Select, then Back. Clicking a row or pin swaps the drawer for the preview and turns the pin gold. “Back to 16 tournaments” returns to the same scroll position.
  9. 9Partner exit. The partner preview has no count and no Register: “Registers on the organizer's site ↗” in navy, never gold.
  10. 10Who’s registered. Players under 18 show as first name and last initial (Priya S., Theo G.); adults in full. Directors and admins see full names. Results, standings and winners keep full names (decision D3 unchanged).
  11. 11Buttons pinned at the bottom. The same set as the list preview: Register, Remind me, Add to calendar, Share, Full event page. The drawer body scrolls; the buttons never leave view.
  12. 12The link holds the view and the selection: view=map and event= are added to the address, so a shared link opens this exact map and preview.

K’s feedback, and where it landed

Second round · applied on this board

  1. 1“I like option 2 for the map view on desktop.” This board. Map options 1 and 3 retire.
  2. 2“Remove the copy link thing.” Gone from the filter line. A shared address still opens the same filters (note 1).
  3. 3“All filter and sort stuff on one intentional line.” Count at the left, the three dropdowns, the applied chips, Clear all, then Sort at the far right; the drawer’s own Sort is gone (notes 1 and 4).
  4. 4“The text under the filters looks out of place.” Now an info note card with an icon, directly under the line (note 2).
  5. 5“Minors’ names abbreviated, full for admin and TD.” Frame C’s preview shows Priya S. and Theo G. with the one-line note (note 10).
  6. 6“I like the buttons at the bottom of the preview.” Same buttons in the drawer preview, pinned to its bottom (note 11).

First round · applied earlier

“I really love the map idea!” kept · A’s rows in the drawer · B’s preview on selection · a bell instead of an alert card or subscription · no drive times or location button · Lafayette Open 2026 shown as a partner event.
