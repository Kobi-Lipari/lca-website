Notes on this board

  1. 1One panel, before and after creation. Every step stays editable once the event is live. A change that would strand paid players stops and says what to do first (lock lines).
  2. 2Basics gains a tagline and a championship title. The tagline is the kicker over the title and in the link preview. The title (championship_title) adds "Past champions ↗" to the sub nav, never a block.
  3. 3Entry limit: Overall limit is the default. One number, today's max_players. Section cards read "9 entered" with no bar, and one waitlist starts when the event is full.
  4. 4Limits by section. A cap per section (sections[].cap; blank means no cap) and an optional overall number, drawn with the 2027 Paul Morphy Open's figures.
  5. 5Fees stay a ladder. Regular is required; Early, Late and the member discount each switch on. The card shows only the tiers set; Regular alone reads "Entry fee $75" once Championship is picked.
  6. 6Schedules, on or off. A second schedule has its own label, rounds with weekday dates and times, and time control (schedules[] and merge_round; the pick is registrations.schedule_id).
  7. 7The merge round. From this round on both schedules are paired as one field; the page's "Round 4 · schedules merge" row comes from it.
  8. 8Side events, on or off. A child tournament (parent_event_id), kind side or meeting, with its own fee and cap, paid in the same checkout. Arrows reorder; Remove asks about entries first.
  9. 9Hotel & travel, on or off. travel_json holds hotel, rate, block deadline, booking link, parking, airport, drive times, between rounds. Off folds the step to one line.
  10. 10Publish holds the share card and the TD override (display_state). Automatic follows the clock; forcing a state shows TDs the "Shown as" tag.
  11. 11Preview column. A schematic of the tournament page. Blocks a step turns on are outlined in navy with its number and On; blocks every event has say Always.
  12. 12Same page, options off. The 2027 Paul Morphy Open: one schedule, limits by section, no side events, no hotel, so the page takes the simple form.
