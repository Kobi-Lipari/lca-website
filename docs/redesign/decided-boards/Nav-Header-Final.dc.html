15The public never sees a strip. During a live round the Tournaments item carries a worded “Live” tag (named “Tournaments, 1 event live now”) and the homepage hero goes live.
16Dark: the header stays #1a2744; menus turn #1a2744 with #e8ecf4 text and the current row at rgba(255,255,255,.1); the light strip becomes #1a2744 with a 1px #c8a94a rule; live tokens are unchanged.

Q13The strip takes the live colour tokens (not the live fonts) while a round is live. Default: yes.
Q15H5’s brand-gold band becomes a light gold tint before and after the event, and the live tokens during rounds. Default: yes.
