Notes on this board
1Official logo on a 44px cream plate (D1: never recoloured), with “Louisiana Chess / Association” from 1280px. The link is named “Louisiana Chess Association, home”; the image is aria-hidden.
2Six sections from nav.ts, one label per destination. The current one is light gold #e6cf86 with a 2px gold underline and aria-current="page".
3Search is a field-shaped button, 16px left of the utilities: 220px with a ⌘K hint (Ctrl K on Windows and Linux) from 1280px, magnifier plus the word from 1024 to 1279px. An <a href="/search"> underneath, so it works without JavaScript.
4One utility order on every page: Donate · Log in · Join LCA (gold fill, navy text).
5At 1024px the two name lines hide and Search shrinks to icon plus word. Measured here, 12px section padding is too wide at 1280px once Renew or the Live tag shows, so 8px padding and a 12px utility gap run up to 1439px. Frame B has the numbers.
6About opens on click with a caret: Enter, Space, Esc, arrow keys, closes on an outside click. Current row #f3eedc, title navy 600, “You are here” 13px/600 in #705718 (5.6:1). Footer row “LCA on Facebook ↗”; no Facebook icon in the bar.
7Signed in: Donate · My LCA ▾ with the to-do count (the same count as the phone tab). Menu order: to-dos, My LCA, the active event line, Family, Membership card, tools by role, Log out.
8Join LCA hides for active members. Within 30 days of expiry, or once lapsed, a gold Renew takes the Join slot.
9After 80px of scroll the bar condenses to 56px and the rook mark replaces the logo. In round states the strip stays sticky under it at 44px; --header-h feeds scroll-padding-top so focus is never hidden.
10The strip is personal: a signed-in player (waitlisted too), guardian, the event’s TDs or lca_admin, for LCA-run events only, from 6 days before day 1 until 48 hours after the last day. One strip per person: TD, then admin, then player, then guardian.
11Before the event, at check-in and after the final: light gold tint #fbf7e9, navy text, 1px #866a1e rule, 52px. It scrolls away with the page.
12While a round is live: live colour tokens #0e1526 / #f3f5f9, a 2px gold top rule, a “Live · Round 3” tag in #ff4757 with #070b14 text. Sticky. Geist stays; Barlow never loads here.
13One line: status tag in words · event name · the viewer’s sentence · primary button · secondary links · a 44×44 button named “Hide until the next update” (keyed event:phase:round; a new round brings it back). Absolute times and “Updated 6:41 PM”, no countdown.
14TD and admin lines end with “Players see:”, built by the same copy builder with the player role. Admins get the TD’s own button: one console for both (D6).
15The public never sees a strip. During a live round the Tournaments item carries a worded “Live” tag (named “Tournaments, 1 event live now”) and the homepage hero goes live.
16Dark: the header stays #1a2744; menus turn #1a2744 with #e8ecf4 text and the current row at rgba(255,255,255,.1); the light strip becomes #1a2744 with a 1px #c8a94a rule; live tokens are unchanged.
Open questions for K · replan section 6
Q1Header search as a field-shaped button that opens a panel, with the full box only on the homepage, not H4’s two-row header. Default: the button.
Q2During a live LCA round the public sees only the worded “Live” tag on Tournaments and the homepage live hero, not the brief’s public bar. Default: no public bar.
Q3The homepage live hero stays public; the strip and phone pop-ups stay for involved people. Default: public hero, with “You’re on board 6” added only for signed-in involved people.
Q9The official logo sits on a small cream plate inside the navy bar. Default: cream plate, judged by eye on the preview.
Q13The strip takes the live colour tokens (not the live fonts) while a round is live. Default: yes.
Q15H5’s brand-gold band becomes a light gold tint before and after the event, and the live tokens during rounds. Default: yes.
Q16H5’s “in 19 min” countdown is dropped for the absolute time (“starts 7:00 PM”). Default: drop it.

Each default is what this board draws. Answer by number; the board changes only where an answer differs.
