About this option · Directory cards
1Region picker: the map and the region list filter the cards together. The full Google map is one click away (Cards / Map), never in a pop-up.
2Quick chips with live counts plus a three-way sort: A–Z, meets soonest, nearest. Filters live in the URL, so "Kids welcome" can be shared as a link.
3A–Z jump bar. Letters with no clubs are greyed out and skipped by the keyboard.
4Card order: identity (logo slot or initials, club colour as a dot only), then when, where, the time control in plain words, and up to three tags.
5Honest gaps: the 7 clubs without a schedule say "Schedule not listed" and offer "Tell us", instead of a blank or "See club for details".
6Freshness loop: every card carries a "Confirmed" month; reps re-confirm in three steps; visitors can report outdated info.
