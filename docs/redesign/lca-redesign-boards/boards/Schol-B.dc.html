Send a Louisiana kid to Nationals

How the fund works

  1. Kids qualify through LCA's TOC Qualifier in February, or by entering a national scholastic championship.
  2. The board sets stipend amounts each spring and announces them under Board updates.
  3. Every award appears in the treasurer's report. A child's name is shown only with a parent's OK.
See past treasurer's reports

Gifts are processed securely by card, with an emailed receipt.
