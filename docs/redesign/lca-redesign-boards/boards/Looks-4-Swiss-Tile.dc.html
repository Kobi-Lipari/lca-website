The idea

The 8-column page is a chessboard: sections are addressed like squares, with a–h / 1–8 along the edges as wayfinding. Wide, heavy Archivo headlines sit flush left on flat navy, gold and paper, with no gradients, shadows or curves.

Best for

A deliberate rebrand: one system that also drives tournament posters, link-preview images and results tables.

Watch out for

The most systematic of the seven, it can read cold, so test it with older members first. Wide type needs short headlines, and the green, yellow and purple logo can't live here: the mark goes one-color.

Do & don't

Do

Address sections like squares ("c4 · Clubs") and keep the a–h / 1–8 coordinates quiet: Fragment Mono, 12px, muted.

Do

Use gold only as a solid field under black (8.54) or navy (6.51) type; gold-toned text on paper is #866a1e.

Don't

Round corners, add shadows or gradients, or set a checkerboard behind text. The system is flat and square.

Don't

Use signal red for anything but live rounds and alerts, or let color carry a status alone: every tag has words.
