What the research says

Ten findings that shaped every page of options

1
The homepage slideshow fails WCAG Level A. It advances every 5 seconds with no pause control, so every home option replaces it.WCAG 2.2.2 · current HeroSlideshow
2
Gold text on white is 2.28:1. Gold stays the accent and text color on navy (6.51:1). Text on light grounds uses dark gold #866a1e (5.13:1).Computed WCAG ratios · ~135 uses of gold text in the code
3
On phones, Register appears after the whole player list. Every event option puts a price and a Register button near the top.Current TournamentDetailPage
4
A dated list beats a month grid for finding dates. So the calendar becomes a navigator for the list, not a separate view.Hund, Dowell & Mueller 2014 · Luma, Resident Advisor
5
Put the map beside the list. With the map beside the list, 95% of testers used it; on list-only sites 65% never found the map.Baymard
6
Forced accounts cost registrations. They cause 18% of checkout abandonment (24% in 2022) and surprise costs cause 40%. So: guest entry, the full total up front, one checkout per family.Baymard
7
Volunteer listings go stale. 7 of 25 clubs show no schedule today. "Last confirmed" stamps and "Report outdated info" keep listings honest.NC Chess Association · LCA club data
8
Say where the money goes. Only 4% of nonprofit sites do, and the site has no visible Donate entry point today.NN/g nonprofit study
9
Keep desktop navigation visible. Hidden menus were used 27% of the time against about 50% for visible links. Audience labels belong on the homepage as doors, not in the main nav.NN/g · GOV.UK
10
Tournament day happens on phones, in a crowd. Find my name, pin my board, show "updated 2 min ago", and put QR posters on the wall instead of printouts.Chess67 · lichess · parkrun

The big bets

Wide-reaching changes, sized for a coding agent, not a weekend

Pages 5 · 7 · 15

An event-day system

Live mode for every LCA event: round-ready alerts, find my board, "my players" for parents, a hall TV view, and a QR print kit. All of it runs off the pairing engine the site already has.

Pages 6 · 14

Household accounts and one checkout

A guardian registers themselves and their children in one payment. Eligibility is confirmed with a checkbox instead of asking a child's grade. US Chess IDs are looked up inline.

Page 1

One design system, accessible by default

Tokens, an accessible dark gold, a real dark/live theme, a monospace face for chess data, ½ glyphs and status shown in words. This fixes the Level A issues site-wide.

Pages 7 · 11 · 12

A results archive with player pages

Every finished event gets standings, a crosstable, prize winners and a recap. Champions and history build up into content people share.

Pages 8 · 9 · 15

Clubs as living listings

Structured meeting schedules drive a statewide "where to play this week" view, and a freshness loop nudges club reps to confirm their details.

Pages 4 · 5

Partner events as first-class pages

Gulf South clearinghouse events get internal pages with a consistent facts card. Server-rendered link previews make every event share cleanly on Facebook.

Pages 13 · 14

Membership that does work

An eligibility-first join flow, separate renewal, "Am I a member?", a wallet card with a QR for check-in, and giving tied to a purpose.

Real data throughout: events from the live Gulf South feed, the 25 clubs and their meeting times, membership prices. Anything invented is badged Sample data. Prepared for K · October 2026
