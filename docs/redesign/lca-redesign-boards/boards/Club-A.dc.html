Your first visit

What a Monday at Turtle Bay is like, from the stairs to the last round.

  1. 6:45 PM

    Climb the stairs and sign in

    Give your name and US Chess ID at the sign-in table, and pay the $1–2 entry.

  2. 7:00 PM

    Round 1 starts

    You're paired with another player. Each of you has 15 minutes for the game.

  3. Each round

    Report your result, then play again

    Tell the tournament director (TD) the result. The next pairings follow.

  4. Last round

    A casual game, or home

    Stay for a casual game or head out. Either is fine on your first night.
