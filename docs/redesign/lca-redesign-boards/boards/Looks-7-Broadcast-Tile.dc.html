Broadcast Night

Live mode

The idea

A dark, data-first theme borrowed from elite chess broadcasts: player bars, clock bars, board numbers and a results ticker, with gold as the live accent on navy-black. It switches on for event days, so pairings and standings stay readable on a phone in a dim hall, and the same page can go up on the wall as a hall display.

Best for

Tournament-day pages: live pairings, standings and crosstables, round-ready alerts, the check-in desk and the hall TV view of the pairings page.

Watch out for

Long reading tires on dark, so news, bylaws and forms stay light. Never white text on live red (3.34). No engine bars on games in progress. Factual broadcast tone, not esports hype.

9Do and don't

Do

Lead with status: LIVE and the round come before the event title, as on Lichess broadcasts.

Do

Put dark text on live red (5.90), or switch to #c81e32 when the text must be white (5.69).

Don't

Show an engine evaluation bar on a game still in progress. Finished, annotated games only.

Don't

Go esports: no hype copy, glows or flashing. Numbers roll and rows re-sort quietly.
