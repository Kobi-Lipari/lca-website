3 · Chess words, in plain English

The words on the pairing sheet

Full glossary
G/30+5
30 minutes on each clock, plus 5 seconds per move. Up to about 70 minutes a game.
Section
The group your child plays in, set by grade (K–3, K–5…) or by rating.
Swiss
Nobody is knocked out. Each round, kids with similar scores play each other.
Pairings
Who plays whom, on which board, and who has White (White moves first).
Bye
A round with no game. Ask for a half-point bye when you register if you'll miss one.
Rated
Games count toward an official US Chess rating. Needs a US Chess membership.
Touch-move
Touch your own piece on purpose and you must move it. Say “I adjust” to straighten one.
TD
Tournament director, the referee. Kids raise a hand for the TD.

7 · Questions parents ask

Before your first event

My child has never played in a tournament. Is that OK?

Yes. Scholastic events are full of first-timers. Unrated players can enter every grade section, and nobody is knocked out, so your child plays all 5 games.

Does my child need a US Chess membership?

Only for events marked “US Chess rated”. Add the ID once to your family profile; LCA checks it every night and warns you if it expires before an event.

Can I register two or three kids at once?

Yes. Add your children to your LCA account (they don't need logins), then register everyone in one checkout, each in their own section.

Can I watch the games?

Parents wait outside the playing room during rounds. It keeps play quiet and fair. Ask the TD whether parents may look in at the start of a round.

What if my child loses every game?

It happens, and it's how everyone starts. Your child still plays every round. Ask about one good move on the drive home.

We have to leave early. What do we do?

Ask for a bye for the rounds you'll miss when you register, or tell the TD before you go, so no one waits at an empty board.

When do results and ratings appear?

Standings post here the same day. For rated events, the new rating appears at US Chess after the TD reports the event.

Still unsure? Ask a person

Email LouisianaChess@gmail.com and a scholastic volunteer will help you pick an event and a section.
