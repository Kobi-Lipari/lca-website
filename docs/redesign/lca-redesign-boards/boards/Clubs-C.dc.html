Notes on this option

1"Find my region" uses location once, or a typed city. Region chips and the overview map jump to each section.

2Each mini-map is cropped to its region. Hollow pins mean "schedule not listed", and programs with no single venue get no pin.

3Every region shows its board seat: the rep with a Message button, or "Seat open" with Volunteer. Open seats also flag listings that need a check.

4"Get updates" emails you when a club or tournament is added in that region.

5Upcoming tournaments for each region come from LCA events and the Gulf South clearinghouse feed.
