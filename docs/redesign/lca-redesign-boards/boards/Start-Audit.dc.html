Fix first

Accessibility and mobile debt to clear before any visual polish

1High

The slideshow can't be paused

Every 5 s

WCAG 2.2.2 Pause, stop, hide · Level A

HeroSlideshow auto-advances with no pause control, ignores the reduce-motion setting, and its dots are 6×6 px.

Fix: a still "next event" hero that switches to "pairings are posted" while an event runs.

src/pages/HomePage.tsx · HeroSlideshow
2High

The focus ring is nearly invisible

1.54:1

WCAG 1.4.11 Non-text contrast · Level AA

The global outline (outline-ring/50) and the button ring both measure 1.54:1, so keyboard users lose their place.

Fix: a solid 2 px navy outline with a 2 px gap on light grounds; 2 px gold on navy (6.51:1).

src/index.css · components/ui/button.tsx
3High

Gold text on white

2.28:1

WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (minimum) · Level AA

Gold carries meaning on white: club-colour officer titles, news dates and contact links (gold is the default club colour), club initials, the "Opening soon" dot, the "Pinned" icon.

Fix: gold as fill or accent only; a gold-ink token (#7a5c00) for gold-toned text; gold text only on navy.

ClubDetailPage.tsx · ClubsPage.tsx · NewsPage.tsx
4High

Status shown by colour alone

Dot + hover

WCAG 1.4.1 Use of colour · Level A

Home and Tournaments show registration status as a small coloured dot; the words exist only as a hover title.

Fix: words plus colour ("Open", "Opens soon"); darken the green to emerald-700 (5.48:1).

StatusBadge.tsx (StatusDot) · HomePage.tsx
5High

Register is out of reach on phones

Below the list

Mobile · the page's main action

The registration card lives in the sidebar, which renders after the whole player list on phones; there is no sticky register button.

Fix: a sticky bottom bar ("Register · from $40" / "Registered · View"), hidden while the keyboard is open.
