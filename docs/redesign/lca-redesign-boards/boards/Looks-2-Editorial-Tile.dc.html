Editorial Sports

Results as news — every tournament gets its match report.

The idea

Every tournament gets a match report: a big serif headline, one strong photo, a one-line summary, then standings, upsets and rating movers as side modules. The homepage becomes a front page — a lead story for the next or just-finished event, a results ticker and recurring sections such as “Upset of the Month”.

Best for

The homepage, news and results — anywhere LCA has a photo and a story. It finally puts the tournament photos LCA already has to work.

Watch out for

Someone has to write a headline after each event; without a volunteer editor it reads like a stale paper. Every photo needs a credit.

09

Do & don’t

Do

Give every event a headline, a one-line dek and a results box beside the story — standings are the facts, the headline is the news.

Do

Credit every photo, crop tight on people and use one fixed 16:9 crop for every event tile.

Don’t

Wash the lead photo in navy or set text over faces — natural color, caption underneath.

Don’t

Color a rating loss red or lean on arrow glyphs (these fonts lack them) — lead with the sign: +24, −12.
