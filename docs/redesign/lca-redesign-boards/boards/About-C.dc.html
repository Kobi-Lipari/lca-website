Article VI

Regional representatives

In plain words

Each of Louisiana's seven regions gets a seat on the board. If a seat sits empty, the board can now fill it until the next election instead of leaving it open for a year.

§ VI.1 · Seats

The board includes one representative for each region: North Louisiana, Central Louisiana, North of Lake Pontchartrain, New Orleans Metro, Southwest Louisiana, South Central Louisiana and the Bayou Region.

§ VI.2 · Vacancies

Amended Sun, Sep 6, 2026

If a seat is vacant, the board may appoint a member who lives in that region to serve until the next annual election.

Article VII

Meetings

In plain words

Members meet once a year at the State Championship. The board meets through the year, in person or online.

§ VII.1 · Annual meeting

The annual business meeting is held during the Louisiana State Championship on Labor Day weekend. Notice goes to members at least [number] days before.

§ VII.2 · Board meetings and quorum

The board meets at least [number] times a year. [Number] board members, including at least one officer, make a quorum.

§ VII.3 · Online meetings

Added Sun, Sep 6, 2026

Board meetings may be held online. Votes taken online are recorded in the minutes by name.

Design notes
1 · Sticky contents follows the reader; the article on screen is highlighted. On phones it folds into a Contents button.
2 · Every amended section shows a redline (removed / added) with the motion and vote that changed it.
3 · The web page is the official text; PDF is a generated download, not the default view.
4 · Change log in Keep a Changelog style: Added, Changed, Removed, Fixed, one entry per version.
5 · One search covers minutes, bylaws and rules, with the matching sentence shown in the row.
6 · Decisions register: each motion is stored once and linked to its minutes and the section it changed.
