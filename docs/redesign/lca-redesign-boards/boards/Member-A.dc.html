Design notes
1 · Two questions pick the tier. Yes/no only: no birthdates, no grades.
2 · Household builder prices every combination and recommends the cheapest; ties go to Family.
3 · The family math is printed openly: Family only saves money with three children.
4 · Join and Renew are separate paths; renewing never asks you to start over.
5 · Status lookup by US Chess ID or email; shows first name and last initial only.
6 · Pay first, set a password on the confirmation page (today's site sends visitors to sign up before they can choose).
