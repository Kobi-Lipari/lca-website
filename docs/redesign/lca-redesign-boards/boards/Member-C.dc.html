Design notes
1 · Purpose first: three funds, each with what it does and its progress toward a goal.
2 · Four preset amounts plus Other (numeric keypad), once or monthly, and a board-approved impact line for the chosen amount.
3 · Money in and out per fund, linked to the treasurer's report. Few nonprofits say where gifts go.
4 · Sponsored entries connect gifts to registration; requests are private and approved by an officer.
5 · One optional add-on at LCA checkouts, never pre-checked.
6 · The thank-you wall is opt-in and never shows amounts. Donate also appears in the header, the footer, every confirmation page and the Scholastic page.
