New to tournaments?

Classical · Quick · Blitz
How long games last. Classical runs one or two long days, Quick about an afternoon, Blitz a few minutes per player.
G/90+30
Each player has 90 minutes, and 30 seconds are added after every move.
Section
You play people near your strength. U1400 means players rated under 1400.
US Chess rated
Results count toward your rating, so you'll need a US Chess membership.
Partner event
Run by another organizer. You enter and pay on their site.

Notes on this option

1One row of filter chips, worded the way players talk. A chip that's on shows its value ("When: Next 3 months"); each opens a short multi-select.

2What's applied is always visible: removable chips, the live count and Clear all. Filters live in the URL, so "Copy link" shares this exact view.

3LCA rows give status in words, "X of Y registered" with a fill bar and the deadline. Register is the only gold button in a row.

4Partner rows are lighter and invent nothing: the feed has no sections, fees or status, so the row says where entry happens and flags the new tab.

5Month headings carry counts and pin under the filter bar while you scroll; "This weekend" gets its own sub-heading.

6One calendar feed (.ics or Google) that follows the current filters, with an LCA-events-only option.
