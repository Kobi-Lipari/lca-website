Heritage Club

since 1915 — the members’ club room, set in type

The idea

LCA as a members’ club room, not a startup: cream paper, navy ink, and gold kept for rules, seals and the one important thing on a page. The identity comes from type — Caslon headlines, engraved small caps, thick–thin rules — so results read like a printed program or an honor board.

Best for

Governance, champions and membership — and a calm, trustworthy base for the whole site, with Broadcast Night as the live theme.

Watch out for

Fast-changing live data, and costume. Heritage comes from type, not parchment or props — and brand gold is never text on cream (2.05:1).

09

Do & don’t

Do

Let type carry the heritage: one Caslon headline, small-caps labels and a single thick–thin rule per section.

Do

Set gold-toned text and links in #705718. Keep brand gold for rules, seals, fills with navy text, and text on navy.

Don’t

Dress it in costume — parchment textures, script fonts, faux-aged stains or a wax seal on every card.

Don’t

Set ratings, scores or tables in Caslon Display — it has no tabular figures. Stacked numbers go in Source Serif 4 or Plex Mono.
