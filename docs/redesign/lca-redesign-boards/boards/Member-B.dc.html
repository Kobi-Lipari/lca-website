Ask us: LouisianaChess@gmail.com

Is this the same as US Chess membership?

No. US Chess membership lets you play rated games anywhere in the country, and you buy it from US Chess. LCA membership is Louisiana's state association: it supports chess here and gets you the member price at LCA events.

When does my membership end?

[Board to confirm: 12 months from the day you join, or December 31 each year.] We email you 30 days and 7 days before it ends.

Design notes
1 · One highlighted card with a label that is literally true ("Best value with 3 children"), never "Most popular".
2 · Dues breakdown with placeholders until the treasurer enters real figures; links to the report.
3 · Auto-renew is opt-in, with a heads-up email a week before any charge.
4 · Registration checks the membership against the event date and offers renewal in the same checkout.
5 · Home club at checkout gives clubs a member count and reps a renewal list.
6 · The FAQ leads with the most common confusion: LCA vs US Chess membership.
