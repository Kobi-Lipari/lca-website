Good to know

Full rules and FAQ

Time control

G/90+30: 90 minutes each, plus 30 seconds after every move. A game can run about 4 hours.

US Chess membership

Required, and current on Jun 12. We check your US Chess ID as you enter and warn you if it lapses.

Byes

Can't make a round? Take a ½-point bye in Rounds 1–4. Ask when you register or before Round 2.

Arriving

Check in by 9:30 AM Saturday. Not at your board 30 minutes after a round starts? That game is a forfeit.

Withdrawing

Full refund until Wed, Jun 9. After that, your fee becomes credit toward another LCA event.

Discounts

LCA members pay $10 less. Codes: FAMILY for siblings; LUNCH, $10 off for free or reduced-price lunch.
