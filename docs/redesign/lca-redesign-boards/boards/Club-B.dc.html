Notes on this option

1The club rep sets the colour and uploads the logo. The colour appears only as a band, a light tint, heading bars and dots, never as text.

2Follow sends a short email when the club posts news or adds an event. Add to calendar subscribes to both weekly nights.

3The tabs jump to sections of one page, so a link like /clubs/baton-rouge#events can be shared.

4Club-run events use LCA registration and show "X of Y registered". Partner events link to the organizer's site.

5The leaderboard is built from LCA's results archive, and players can opt out.

6The contact form relays messages, so no rep's email address is ever published.
