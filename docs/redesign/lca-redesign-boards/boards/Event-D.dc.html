Why Morphy, why New Orleans 3

The festival carries a New Orleans name.

Paul Morphy was born in New Orleans in 1837. He won the First American Chess Congress in 1857, and in Paris in 1858 he played the "Opera Game", a 17-move win players still study. Every June, Louisiana plays in his name.
