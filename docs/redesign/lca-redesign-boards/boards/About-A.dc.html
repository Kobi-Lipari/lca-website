About LCASample data · meeting dates, recap, seats, bylaw articles

A volunteer association that does its business in the open

The Louisiana Chess Association is the state's US Chess affiliate. Volunteers have served Louisiana players since 1915: we run the state championships, list tournaments across the Gulf South and support clubs in all seven regions.
