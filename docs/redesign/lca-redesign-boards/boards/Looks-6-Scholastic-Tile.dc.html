Bright Scholastic

Every pawn can promote

The idea

A friendly, rounded, colorful skin for the Scholastic pages and parent sign-up, still anchored in LCA navy so it reads as the same association. Each section owns a color so a parent finds their child's section at a glance, and a pawn promotes along a track as a family finishes sign-up.

Best for

The Scholastic hub, parent and family sign-up, section pairings at the State Scholastic Championship, school-club pages. A section theme, not site-wide.

Watch out for

Adults and the board want calmer pages, so keep it scoped. Section hues can clash with status colors, so status always gets words and an icon. Kids' faces only with a signed photo release.

9Do and don't

Do

One color per section, repeated on the tab, the pairings sheet and the confirmation email.

Do

Keep navy as the anchor: text, headings and press-down buttons stay navy, so coaches still see LCA.

Don't

Put white text on gold or gold text on cream. Gold is a fill with navy text; gold-toned words use #866a1e.

Don't

Show a child's face or full name publicly without a signed release. Crop to boards and hands; print "Maya R."
