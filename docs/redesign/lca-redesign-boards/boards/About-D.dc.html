Design notes
1 · Reigning champions band: one source feeds this page and a one-line band on the homepage.
2 · Honor roll filtered by title; ten rows by default, "Show all N years" for the rest; stacks as cards on phones.
3 · Every champion name opens a player page: titles, LCA results, US Chess ID.
4 · The Opera Game (public domain) steps move by move on a real board; the timeline mixes Morphy and LCA history.
5 · Coverage by decade turns missing history into a visible, finishable job.
6 · Member submissions land in a review queue; nothing goes live without an officer's approval.
