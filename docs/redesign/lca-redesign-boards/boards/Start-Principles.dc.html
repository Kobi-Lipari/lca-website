Principles & review checklist

Ten principles behind every option on this board, plus the 39-point design review checklist from the UX evidence, condensed so any option can be checked against it.

How to use it

  • Walk the rows for every area an option touches and mark each one pass, fix or not applicable.
  • Fails todayConfirmed in today's code. It has to pass in whatever ships.

Source: ux-evidence.md (NN/g, Baymard, GOV.UK, USWDS, W3C WAI / WCAG 2.2). Row numbers match its checklist.

Principles

10 · each with an example
  1. 01

    Dated and current — every item shows when

    “Sat–Sun, Oct 10–11” always carries its weekday; live pages say “Updated 9:42 PM”, documents “Last updated”.

    Checks 10 · 11 · 28 · 39
  2. 02

    Built for the venue — a phone, a crowd, bad signal

    Pairings open with “Find my name”, your family's games pinned, a text-size toggle and auto-refresh.

    Checks 9 · 17 · 26 · 28 · 36
  3. 03

    Status in words, never colour alone

    “Registration open”, “Waitlist”, “Live · Round 3”. A coloured dot may sit beside the words, never replace them.

    Checks 13 · 29
  4. 04

    Plain language for parents — explain G/30+5 inline

    “G/30+5 · 30 minutes each, plus 5 seconds a move” on first use; nothing essential hides in a tooltip.

    Checks 37
  5. 05

    One table design for entries, pairings and standings

    Real tables with a caption and a Player column that stays put when you scroll sideways; scores read 3½, not 3.5.

    Checks 26 · 27 · 29
  6. 06

    Collect only what's needed

    An “In 8th grade or below?” checkbox, never a child's grade or birthdate; the US Chess ID lookup means nothing known is asked twice.

    Checks 19 · 20 · 25
  7. 07

    The full price before payment

    Entry fee, early or late pricing and the member discount add up to one total before Stripe, which opens in the same tab.

    Checks 17 · 18 · 23 · 24
  8. 08

    Nothing moves on its own

    The 5-second slideshow (fails WCAG 2.2.2) gives way to a “next event” hero that becomes “Pairings posted” during an event.

    Checks 7 · 8 · 9
  9. 09

    Volunteer-maintainable — freshness stamps, not heroics

    Club listings and documents show “Last updated”, so a stale page is visible and any volunteer can fix it.

    Checks 39
  10. 10

    Gold is an accent; gold text only on navy

    Brand gold #c8a94a is 2.28:1 on white, so on light grounds it is a fill or a rule; gold-toned text uses #866a1e.

    Checks 34 · 35

What the evidence settles

  1. 1Navigation by task and topic, always visible on desktop
  2. 2A “next event” hero replaces the auto-advancing slideshow
  3. 3Tournament list first, removable filter chips, weekday on dates
  4. 4Register visible on phones, full total first, Stripe in the same tab
  5. 5“Find my name”, pinned games and an “Updated” time on pairings
  6. 6Accessibility debt gets fixed before visual polish
  7. 7A visible Donate entry point; membership as “which one fits you”
  8. 8The five hero images weigh about 2.1 MB, a whole median mobile page

Design review checklist

39 checks · 8 areas

Navigation1–6

  • 1Desktop (1024px and wider) shows every top-level item; no hamburger.
  • 27 or fewer top items, task or topic labels (not audiences), current section shown.
  • 3Dropdowns open on click or Enter, show a caret and hold 6 links or fewer, else a mega menu.
  • 4The mobile menu button says “Menu” in text and has a visible border.
  • 5Donate, Log in / Account and Join sit top-right, in the same order on every page.
  • 6Pages three levels deep show a breadcrumb or a back link to their parent.

Homepage7–10

  • 7Nothing moves on its own without a visible pause; no motion with “reduce motion” or on phones.Fails todaySlideshow auto-advances every 5 s with no pause (WCAG 2.2.2, Level A).
  • 8First screen: LCA's identity, three top tasks, and the next event with day, date, city and status.
  • 9On event days, “Pairings posted” is one tap from the homepage.
  • 10Every news or time-sensitive item shows a date.

Tournaments11–16

  • 11List view is the default, and every date includes the day of the week.
  • 12Active filters are removable chips with “Clear all”; the result count is always visible; filters live in the URL.
  • 13Registration status is shown as text, never as a coloured dot alone.Fails todayStatus dots use colour alone.
  • 14External events show the arrow plus “opens in new tab” text.
  • 15LCA events offer .ics and Google Calendar export and include Event markup for Google.
  • 16Past events sit under Results, separate from upcoming events.

Pairings and results26–29

  • 26Pairings, standings and crosstables have “Find my name”; your own and your children's games are pinned.
  • 27Real tables: a caption and scope, a labelled keyboard-reachable scroll area, a fixed Player column.
  • 28Live pages have a text-size toggle, “Updated hh:mm” and auto-refresh.
  • 29The print view uses large type, doesn't depend on colour and never splits rows across pages.

Registration and payment17–25

  • 17On phones, the Register button and price are visible without scrolling to the bottom.Fails todayThe Register card sits below the player list on phones.
  • 18The full total, including fees, is shown before payment.
  • 19Eligibility is asked first, and only necessary information is collected.
  • 20US Chess ID lookup happens inside the form; nothing already known is asked again.
  • 21Several players: a list with Change / Remove for each, then “Check your answers”.
  • 22Errors: a linked summary at the top plus a fix-it message by each field; checked on leaving a field or on submit, not while typing.
  • 23Payment redirects in the same tab, returns to a confirmation page with next steps, and sends an email.
  • 24Withdraw buttons use specific labels and state the refund rule.
  • 25Number fields use type=text with inputmode=numeric; personal fields have autocomplete.

Clubs and maps30–31

  • 30Every map has an equivalent list (the default on phones), and each location has a Directions link.
  • 31Map markers can be reached by keyboard and have names.

Money32–33

  • 32Donate is visible from every page, with preset amounts, Other, a monthly option and where the money goes.Fails todayThere is no visible Donate entry point anywhere.
  • 33Membership is chosen by eligibility; no badge that isn't literally true; the family maths is stated.

Accessibility and quality34–39

  • 34All text at least 4.5:1 (large text 3:1); gold never as text on light grounds; controls and focus rings at least 3:1.Fails todayGold text on light grounds at 2.28:1; small faded white text on navy below 4.5:1.
  • 35The focus ring is a 2px solid line, at least 3:1, never hidden under the sticky header.Fails todayThe focus ring is 1.54:1.
  • 36Tap targets are at least 24px everywhere and 44px for primary mobile actions.
  • 37Jargon is defined inline on first use; essential information never sits only in a tooltip.
  • 38Homepage under 1 MB on mobile; largest paint ≤ 2.5 s, layout shift ≤ 0.1, input response ≤ 200 ms.Fails todayThe five hero images total about 2.1 MB.
  • 39Governance documents show “Last updated”; Contact and Support labels are clearly different.
