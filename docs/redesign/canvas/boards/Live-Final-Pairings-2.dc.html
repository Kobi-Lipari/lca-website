How the queue behaves

  • A report appears in the queue the moment one player sends it. Usually that is the winner, as on board 7.
  • If the opponent sends the same result, the row turns to "Opponent agrees" and Confirm is one tap. A different result turns it to "Reports disagree" and moves it to the top.
  • Nothing posts publicly until a TD confirms. Players see "Reported, awaiting TD" on their own card only.
  • Enter a different result opens this sheet from any row. The players' reports are never edited or deleted; the TD's entry is added under them, final, with the TD's name and time.
  • A confirmed result can still be corrected from the table's Edit link. The old value stays in the history, and both players see the change on their card.
  • Results the TD enters in the console or from a paper sheet skip the queue and post on save.
  • The queue and the Status column are visible only to this event's TDs and LCA admins.
