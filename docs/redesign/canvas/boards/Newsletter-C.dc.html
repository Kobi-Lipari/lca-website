Note for the canvas

What: The issue builds itself from what the site already holds; the leaders only add their words on top. The page is a checklist of six blocks showing what the site put in each, with a box for a note. Untouched, it still goes out complete on the 1st: the least work and never late or empty, but the least voice of the four.

Built from: the six boards named on the left.

K's feedback applied: on the site, edited together by LCA admins, the president and the media manager; out "automatically on the same date and time (unless edited)"; every member email, new members added; opting out stops this list only; no postal address in any email, as K decided.

Open for K:

  1. The send rule: here, it goes out by itself (note 3).
  2. Writing together: turn-taking with history, or everyone typing at once?
  3. The day and time: drawn as the 1st of each month at 8:00 AM Central.
  4. Each issue readable on the site by anyone? Drawn: yes.
  5. Weekly "News by email" (Mondays, 8:00 AM): replaced, or both?
  6. Opens and clicks are not tracked, as drawn.

Notes on this board

  1. 1Most blocks fill themselves: Coming up from the calendar, Results from finished events, From the clubs from club pages, News you may have missed from News. Each card says where.
  2. 2The note is the only part someone writes, and it is optional: the issue goes out without it. Board business stays left out until someone writes in it.
  3. 3The send rule, open for K: it goes out by itself, as it stands. "Hold this issue" stops this month only; a month with nothing at all is not sent and the editors are told.
  4. 4What changed since you last looked: a dated list of what the site added on its own, so nobody is surprised by what goes out (B1).
  5. 5Partner events never get Register. The Ken Ferguson Memorial arrives marked "Partner event · Registers on the organizer's site ↗", with no count.
