LCA Newsletter · The page · Option 4

The letter on the desk

The page looks like the letter itself lying on a desk: you type straight onto it, exactly as members will read it.

Sample data

On a tablet, by touch

The Start from bar and the From row sit at the top. The letter fills the width and every tool is big enough for a finger. Hold a word to select it, then drag the round handles. “See the email” is one button at the top.

On a phone

The same letter sheet fills the screen, with the Start from bar and the From row above large tools. One button at the foot, “See the email”, brings up the email as members will get it. “Back to writing” closes it.
