Rules and data. The list always holds all 25; the map never filters it, only Filters do, and they act on both. Sorts: A to Z, and Nearest once located. Location is read once on tap and kept only in ?near=; a typed city or ZIP is sent only to look up a point. No card carries a computed date, a tonight state or a count; the page never computes a next date, a meets-today count, a Meets soonest order or a freshness badge.

A card reads clubs.meeting_text (the club's own line, written by the club's representative on its club page) and clubs.schedule_status; filters read clubs.region (Appendix C), clubs.weekend_play and clubs.scholastic_friendly, set by the club on its page; miles come from club_distances (hub to club, road, cached) through a ZIP or city to point lookup; Suggest an update and Suggest a club write club_reports to the club's representative with the regional seat holder copied, LCA admins while a seat is open.

Not on this page: Tonight, Next, Checked months, the monthly email, Get updates, a legend panel, a regions overlay, Search this area, club-manager claims, rosters, leaderboards, member counts.
