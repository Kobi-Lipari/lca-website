Notes on this board

1 · Signed out, or not playing

  1. 1Round-ready banner in the page, under the page tabs, in the decided banner style (Nav-Mobile-Final): what is ready, when it was posted, when the round starts. Signed out there is no personal line.
  2. 2Find your board by typing a few letters. Every section at once; matching rows get a tint and the typed letters are marked. The count heads the list at normal size, never squeezed beside the field.
  3. 3A follow star on each row. Colour shows as a shape and a word: a hollow circle for White, a filled one for Black. Pairings carry full names, minors included (D3).
  4. 4No pinned card here. A viewer who is signed out or not registered gets Find your board as the first tab. A line offers Log in, which pins your own board.
  5. 5Round-ready email (1b). Subject, board, colour in words, opponent and start time with the weekday; See your board; one link to stop. Email only, no text messages (D8).

2 · Signed in and playing

  1. 6Your board, pinned and big: “Board 7 · You have White · vs Sam Nguyen 1650 · starts 7:00 PM”, with the minutes left and where the board is. The first tab reads My board.
  2. 7“Updated 2 min ago” is when the live data last changed; the page checks every 20 seconds while it is open (D7), and the button checks now.
  3. 8Text size is one Aa button in the app bar, on the tournament day pages only. It replaces the three A / A+ / A++ buttons.
  4. 9The Aa menu (2b): Text size · Small · Medium · Large, each in its own size, Medium ticked. Saved on this phone.
  5. 10Your rounds so far: result in words (Won, Lost), opponent and rating, colour by shape and word; the current round shows its board. Report your result waits for the round to start.

3 · After the game

  1. 11Report your result appears on the pinned card for a signed-in player registered in the round, once the round starts. Nobody else gets the button.
  2. 12The sheet: I won · Draw · I lost with the score each gives, the opponent named, and “Your TD confirms every result”. One tap on Send.
  3. 13After sending (3b): “You reported: 1–0 · Waiting for the TD”, the status in words “Reported, awaiting TD”, and the opponent’s line “Sam has not reported yet”. Shown to the two players only; changeable until the TD confirms.
  4. 14The TD sees one report at once (3c): who reported, when, whether the opponent agreed, conflicts flagged as “Reports disagree”. Confirm in one tap or enter a different result.
  5. 15Public pairings wait for the TD. Everyone else sees “Board 7 · In play” until the TD confirms; then the result shows, marked Confirmed by TD.

4 · A parent

  1. 16My players, one row per child: board, colour by shape and word, opponent, result with its status in words (“Confirmed by TD”, “In play”) and score. A guardian’s children are followed automatically.
  2. 17Live standings for the child’s section, the child’s row tinted and marked “following”. It moves only on confirmed results.
  3. 18Alerts by email, one per round per address (4b): subject “Round 3 pairings: Maya, Leo”, a line per child (recipientsFor, WS06). Email only.

K’s feedback, and where it landed

Page 7 notes, message 1, Fri, Oct 9, 2026. Item numbers are this board’s, so each can be revised on its own.

  • 7.1 Keep Find your board for people not signed in or not playing.

    Frame 1, badges 1 to 4. It is the first tab whenever there is no board to pin.

  • 7.2 Pin your board when actually playing.

    Frame 2, badges 6, 7 and 10. Board, colour, opponent and start time in big type.

  • 7.3 Text size (A, A+, A++) stays, with a smaller footprint, especially on mobile.

    The Aa menu in the app bar, badges 8 and 9 (frame 2 and inset 2b).

  • 7.4 Report your result, for signed-in players registered in the round.

    Frame 3 and inset 3b, badges 11 to 13.

  • 7.5 The TD sees a report even when only one player (usually the winner) has reported; it steers the TD, never sets the result; easy to check and override.

    Inset 3c and frame 3b, badges 14 and 15. The full queue belongs on the TD console board.

Not on this board: Hall TV with all sections on one screen, Option B’s search text, Option C’s Download menu, the print pairings title and the US Chess report are on the other page 7 boards.
