The rules this list follows

An item is something the viewer can finish or decide today, from this page or one of its rooms, with a deadline within 14 days or a person waiting on the viewer now; it carries exactly one button in words. A status, a count of things that are fine, or information with no action is never an item.

Tiers: 1 due today or already past; 2 money and eligibility with a date, in WS13's order (unpaid entry, US Chess before an entered event, LCA membership within 30 days, a waitlist offer), then the nearer date first; 3 a person waiting on you, longest wait first; 4 dated upkeep, sooner deadline first, undated last; 5 counts and summaries (an admin's queue line only).

Inside a tier: sooner deadline, then the role order You · Family · Director · Representative · Board · Admin.

Cap: seven open; the rest behind "Show N more", and no role may hold more than three rows before the fold.

The header's My LCA count stays the personal count; volunteer items never count there.
