Notes on this option

  1. 1Month rows carry a count and how many register on LCA; they stay pinned under the column header while you scroll.
  2. 2“This weekend” gets its own sub-heading, as on Tourn-A, then “Later in October”.
  3. 3One wide Registration cell: status in words, a 120px bar, “23 of 60 registered”, spots left and the deadline. It answers “should I hurry?”.
  4. 4Partner rows are one light line. Type reads “Not listed” where the feed has nothing.
  5. 5The row-end chevron opens a one-line peek with sections, fees and round times, Register and Full event page.
  6. 6The bell sits right after the event name. Signed out, it says what you’d get and offers Log in or Create an account.

2 · Month-grouped with entry bars

Tourn-A’s look turned into columns: month group headers with counts, heavier two-line LCA rows with a wide inline entry bar, light one-line partner rows, and a one-line peek instead of a full expansion.

K’s feedback applied: Brings A’s way of showing tournaments (month groups with counts, This weekend, weighted LCA rows, light partner rows) into the table. Same toolbar fixes as option 1. No saved-views bar, no subscribe card. The bell sits right next to the name it reminds you about, and this option shows the signed-out path K asked for: log in or create an account.

Best for
Players and parents who liked the List view but want columns and sorting. The inline bar answers “should I hurry?” at a glance.
Trade-off
The tallest of the three and the closest to the List view, so Table adds the least new here. The groups disappear when sorting by any other column. A peek row holds less than a full expansion or a preview.
Other widths
1024–1279px: the bar drops to 80px, Date and Event stay frozen. 768–1023px: the peek wraps to two lines and the bell is always visible. The month header stays sticky under the toolbar at every width.

Month groups show while the table is sorted by date. Sorting by any other column folds them away into flat rows; date returns them.

How the rows are weighted

  • LCA rows are 66px: the date tile, the format line and the registration bar. These are the events people can enter here.
  • Partner rows are 44px and one line: the name, the organizer and where entry happens. The feed carries no sections, fees or counts, so the row invents none.
  • The peek row is one line. For anything more, Full event page.
