1 · Before your first event

What a scholastic Saturday looks like

A typical one-day LCA scholastic: 5 rounds at , nobody is knocked out, and your child plays every round. Parents wait just outside the playing room.

G/30+5 · Each player has 30 minutes on the clock, plus 5 seconds per move. A game can last about 70 minutes.

Chess words, in plain English

The words on the pairing sheet

Full glossary
G/30+5
30 minutes on each clock, plus 5 seconds per move. Up to about 70 minutes a game.
Section
The group your child plays in, set by grade (K–3, K–5 and so on) or by rating.
Swiss
Nobody is knocked out. Each round, kids with similar scores play each other.
Pairings
Who plays whom, on which board, and who has White (White moves first).
Bye
A round with no game. Ask for a ½-point bye when you register if your child will miss one.
Rated
Games count toward an official US Chess rating. Needs a US Chess membership.
Touch-move
Touch your own piece on purpose and you must move it. Say "I adjust" to straighten one.
TD
Tournament director, the referee. Kids raise a hand for the TD.

Safety

How events keep kids safe

  • Parents and coaches wait outside the playing room during games.
  • A tournament director runs every event. Kids raise a hand for help.
  • LCA events follow US Chess rules and its safe-play policy.
Safe-play policy at uschess.org

Privacy

What LCA keeps about your child

We ask for

  • Your child's first and last name
  • US Chess ID, rated events only
  • A yes or no box for grade sections
  • Your email and payment. Kids get no login.

We never ask for

  • Birthdate, grade or school
  • Address, or a child's phone or email

Public: name, section, rating and results on pairings and standings, as at every rated event.

Remove a child any time in My LCA. Results sent to US Chess stay on record.
