Regions first

Louisiana’s seven regions become the map: each region is a selectable shape with its name and count, neighbouring states sit at the map edges as tabs with counts, and the list beside it groups events by region, so gaps in coverage and the clubs that fill them are obvious.

Frame 1440 · signed in · two states

Best for

Louisiana players who think “what is near me” by area (Northshore, Acadiana, Shreveport), and LCA itself, because the empty north and centre are visible at a glance and point people to clubs instead.

Trade-off

Grouping by region rather than by date makes “what is on this weekend” a List-view question. Region shapes need a parish-to-region table that does not exist yet (regions.ts maps clubs, not parishes). Out-of-state events are one click further away.

Feedback applied

Region counts become the map itself; filled versus hollow pins appear inside a chosen region; the list stays beside the map; the bell replaces the alert card; the decided toolbar; no drive times at all; the Region chip and the map are one filter, so the link stays the single source; Lafayette Open shown as a partner.

Other widths and phones

Below 1100px the map shrinks to 420px above the grouped list. On phones: “the list with a small static region map and a Map toggle”, where the region shapes double as big tap targets. Signed in here because the popover shows the signed-in state.
