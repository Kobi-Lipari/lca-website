Winter Open

At a glance

Rounds
Four, at 9:00 AM, 11:30 AM, 2:00 PM and 4:30 PM
Sections
Open, Under 1400, and Scholastic (K to 12)
Entry
$25 through Sat, Jan 2, 2027, then $35
Closes
Fri, Jan 8, 2027 at 8:00 PM, on King Registration

Baton Rouge Chess Club warmly invites you to the Baton Rouge Winter Open, a one-day rated tournament to start the new year.

Players of every level are welcome, and young players have a section of their own. We hope to see you there.

The details

When
Sat, Jan 9, 2027. Rounds at 9:00 AM, 11:30 AM, 2:00 PM and 4:30 PM.
Where
Baton Rouge; the exact place is on the registration page.
Sections
Open, Under 1400, and Scholastic (K to 12).
Rounds and time control
Four rounds, game in 45 minutes with a 5 second delay, US Chess rated.
Entry fee
$25 through Sat, Jan 2, 2027, then $35.
Registration
On King Registration, closes Fri, Jan 8, 2027 at 8:00 PM.
Register on King Registration

If the button does not show, use this link: https://www.kingregistration.com/…

Good to know

Bring a set and a clock if you have them.

A current US Chess membership is needed to play.

Who runs it

Run by Baton Rouge Chess Club. Questions? Reply to this email and it reaches the club.

You get tournament email from LCA because you are an LCA member.
