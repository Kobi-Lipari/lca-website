A note from the president

Dear members,

Thank you for a great year of chess in Louisiana, and to every volunteer who made it happen.

The new year brings more chances to play, to learn and to meet new people at the board.

One ask: please renew your membership, and bring one new player to a club night.

See you over the board,
LCA President
