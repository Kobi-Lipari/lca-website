What
The header, the hero and the identity and search band, drawn once per mode in ladder order. The server picks the first mode whose trigger matches; a mode missing its data falls through, so no mode ever renders an empty frame. Everything below the band (upcoming six, doors, results and champions, news, membership) is the same in every mode and is drawn on Home-Final.

Notes on this board

  1. 1Header: the decided bar with the Search button (Nav-Header-Final). It is identical in every mode; signed in, the utilities read Donate · My LCA.
  2. 2Live colour tokens on the hero container only: #0e1526 ground, #f3f5f9 ink, a "Live" tag in #ff4757 with #070b14 text. Type stays Geist, no live fonts on the homepage, no photo. The dot is static; the word "Live" carries the meaning.
  3. 3Personal line, added on the client from useEventMode() for signed-in involved people only, so the hero and the header strip never disagree. The dashed outline marks a personal line on this board; it is not drawn on the site.
  4. 4Round row in words as an ordered list, never a bare dot and never an abbreviated round. At 390px it compacts to "1 Final · 2 Final · 3 Live · 4 · 5" under a visible "Rounds" heading.
  5. 5An absolute "Updated" time and a Pause updates button (WCAG 2.2.2). The hero polls every 20 seconds and stops while the tab is hidden.
  6. 6Identity and search band: the same fixed place under every mode, so the box never moves between visits and search never becomes the hero. Quick-pick chips stay hidden until WS04 and WS09 ship their filters. One gold primary per hero: the band's Search button and the header's Join LCA are site chrome, the same in every mode, as on Home-Final.
  7. 7Event day keeps LCA's own event on the front page once online entry has closed. The hall photo appears on LCA-run modes only.
  8. 8One gold primary, set by registration state. The four swatches are the four states; only one ever shows. Last call outranks Results because a deadline is actionable.
  9. 9Results: the Heritage accent only (thick–thin rule and seal) in Geist. Winners by full name with ½; names never link to a player page (D9). After four sections, "+2 more sections".
  10. 10Announced: signed in gets the reminder; signed out gets Event details and a calendar file that collects nothing, never "Log in to get a reminder". No venue is stored yet, so the city line is dropped, not printed as a placeholder.
  11. 11Grid motif (a–h, 8–1, 0 KB) on partner and quiet modes. A partner event never wears LCA's hall photo.
  12. 12Partner rows: the "Partner event" tag and "Registers on the organizer's site ↗", never Register and never a count. They drop out when the feed is more than 3 days old.
  13. 13The Next LCA event line ends every non-LCA mode, status in words. It is omitted when nothing is announced within 180 days, as on the Week frame (Mon, Oct 5), and on the Clubs floor, which has no event data.
  14. 14Quiet is the usual homepage, so it carries real dated events however far away, and clubs, which meet every week and suit beginners and kids.
  15. 15Clubs floor: never blank. It is also what the client shows when /api/home fails, drawn from the static regions list with no error message.

Open questions for K

  1. Q3 · Live hero stays publicThe homepage live hero is public; the header strip and phone pop-ups stay for involved people. Default: public hero, with "You're on board 6" only for signed-in involved people.
  2. Q11 · A 30-day registration windowRegister mode starts 30 days out, with a last-call tier at 14 days and early-deadline overrides at 14 and 7 days, instead of the brief's 21 days. Default: yes.
  3. Q14 · Results ship in Phase 0The Results hero and the Champions band ship from today's standings and state champions, ahead of WS10's archive; Results switches to WS10's snapshot when it lands. Default: yes, behind homeResults and homeChampions.
