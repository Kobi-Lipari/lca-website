All on one screen Not available

46 boards will not fit on one screen at a size people can read from across the room. The most it holds is 16.

How the suggestion is made

  • 30 players or fewer: All on one screen is suggested.
  • More than 30 players, or more than 16 boards in a round: One at a time is suggested.
  • More than 16 boards: All on one screen is not available, and the card says why.
  • Withdrawals and late entries can change the count; the suggestion follows, but never switches the TV by itself once the TD has chosen.
