21Bylaws reader, contents as a sheet

The contents rail becomes a bar pinned under the tabs that names the article on screen. Tapping it opens this sheet; picking an article closes it and scrolls there.

The sheet also holds the change log (one tap away, with how many changes the current version made) and the PDF download. Close or a swipe down returns to the text.

"In plain words" boxes and redlines stay in the text, full width.
