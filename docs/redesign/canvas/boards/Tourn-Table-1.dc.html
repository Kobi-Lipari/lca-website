Notes on this board

  • Same toolbar on every view: Upcoming | Results, the centred view switcher with the search on its right, chips When · Type · Region, applied chips with a live count, Clear all and Copy link to this view.
  • LCA rows say “LCA event”, give status in words and “X of Y registered”. Partner rows say “Partner event” and “Registers on the organizer’s site ↗”, never Register and never a count. Lafayette Open 2026 is a partner event, as on the decided List board.
  • The bell appears on a row on hover and opens the reminder options: one week and one day before ticked, plus when registration opens and before the early price ends. Signed out, it asks you to log in or create an account.

  1. 1The toolbar from the shared notes. No Rated chip, no LCA-run or Partner chip, no saved views.
  2. 2Every header is a sort button; only the active one shows its arrow (Date, earliest first, aria-sort ascending). Date and Event stay frozen.
  3. 3One Columns control holds the extras (Organizer, Sections, Rating system, Venue). No density, CSV, .ics or batch tools.
  4. 4One row opens in place: sections and fees, format, schedule, venue, prizes and the single gold Register.
  5. 5Partner rows merge Entries and Status into one honest cell. No count, no status tag, no Register.
  6. 6The bell in the last column opens the reminder popover. A filled bell means reminders are already on.

2Month-grouped with entry bars

  1. 1Month rows carry a count and how many register on LCA; they stay pinned under the column header while you scroll.
  2. 2“This weekend” gets its own sub-heading, as on Tourn-A, then “Later in October”.

  4. 4Partner rows are one light line. Type reads “Not listed” where the feed has nothing.
  5. 5The row-end chevron opens a one-line peek with sections, fees and round times, Register and Full event page.
  6. 6The bell sits right after the event name. Signed out, it says what you’d get and offers Log in or Create an account.

  1. 1J and K move the selection, Enter opens the full event page, Esc clears it.
  2. 2A gutter before the date: the bell appears there on hover, like a spreadsheet row handle. A filled bell stays where reminders are on.
  3. 3Source gets its own narrow column so the 13px rows stay one line.
  4. 4The picked row gets a cream fill and a 2px gold ring (as on Tourn-B) and fills the panel. Nothing expands.
  5. 5Reminder options sit inline in the panel instead of a popover, a week and a day ticked.

1 · Clean six

A plain six-column table (Date, Event, City, Type, Entries, Status) with frozen Date and Event, the sort shown in the header, one row expanded in place, and a single Columns control for everything else.

K’s feedback applied: Removes Tourn-E’s saved-views bar, the Rated and LCA-run/Partner chips, the density toggle, CSV and .ics buttons, batch checkboxes and the date-clash panel. Keeps A’s Upcoming | Results selector, centres the view switcher with the search on its right, and replaces calendar subscribing and email alerts with a hover bell. Signed in as a valid member: My LCA in place of Log in, no Renew.

Best for
Regulars who want to scan and sort the whole season fast, and K’s “power table, but not convoluted” baseline. It is the closest to a familiar spreadsheet with the least on screen.
Trade-off
The expanded row pushes the rows below it down. Organizer, sections and venue are one click away in Columns rather than visible, and the table looks the least like the List view K liked.
Other widths
1024–1279px: six columns kept, Date and Event frozen, the rest scrolls sideways (states strip). 768–1023px: the bell column is always visible. Phones get their own Table options later.
