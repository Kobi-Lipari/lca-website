Notes on this board

  1. 1The index is the directory: 21 towns, A to Z, each with its club count, all on one screen. A row opens to its clubs; one town is open at a time. "Show all 25 clubs" opens every town, so the drawer becomes the full list under town headers. Nothing depends on the viewport.
  2. 2Map and index are the same thing: every pin wears its town name as a button. Click a town on the map or in the drawer and the row opens and the pin turns gold. No popover, no pin numbers; at state zoom Baton Rouge · 2 and New Orleans area · 8 are count circles.
  3. 3Near me reads location once, on tap, keeps it only in the address (?near=70808), sorts the towns by distance, opens the nearest and zooms the map. ✕ on the pill or ⌂ All of Louisiana is the way back.
  4. 4No sort control, no counts of who meets when. A card is the club's meeting line in its own words, or Schedule not listed. Filters (region, plays on weekends, scholastic friendly, only if the club says so) hide behind one button; a filtered index greys the towns with nothing left.
  5. 5Who keeps a listing is said once per region, below the fold: the club's own representative writes it, the region's representative is the backstop, and Suggest an update reaches them. No claim path, no roster, no leaderboard, no member counts.
