A note from the president

Dear members,

Thank you to every volunteer who ran the State Championship weekend in Sulphur.

The new year starts busy: the Ken Ferguson Memorial in January, a youth meet every month to May.

One ask: please renew your membership, and bring one new player to a club night.

See you over the board,
LCA President
