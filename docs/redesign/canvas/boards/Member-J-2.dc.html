Who counts as a child on the Family membership?

A K–12 student you're the parent or guardian of, added by name in My LCA with a US Chess ID if they have one. We never ask for a grade, a birthdate or a child's email. Family covers you and up to 3; a 4th is a Scholastic membership at $5 in the same payment.
