Notes on this board

  1. 1Round tabs, section chips with player counts, By board or A–Z. Kept from Live-B. Each choice is in the address (round=, section=, order=), so a shared link opens the same view.
  2. 2Type-to-find highlights the row, nothing else. Matching rows get a gold edge, a search mark and the letters marked; a collapsed section that holds a match opens, and the page scrolls to the first one. No result text sits beside the box. Enter jumps to the next match.
  3. 3Text size is one small "Aa" menu, 44px, at the end of the first controls line. It opens Small · Medium · Large, is remembered on this device, and is the same menu on phones.
  4. 4"Updated 1 min ago" and Refresh share the first line with Round and Order, so the controls card is two lines, not three. The page checks every 60 seconds.
  5. 5"Your game" is pinned only for a signed-in player in this round, with Report your result. Signed out, or signed in but not playing, there is no card: the find box does the work (the phones board keeps "Find your board").
  6. 6The public sees a result only once the TD confirms it. Until then the cell is blank, whether the game is still going or a player has reported. Section progress counts confirmed results only.
  7. 7Reporting, from the player's side. Three choices, then "Reported, awaiting TD" on the player's own card, which only the two players see. The opponent can agree or report something else. Either can change a report until the TD confirms.
  8. 8Byes in words under each table: requested bye, ½ point; pairing bye for an odd number, 1 point.
  9. 9Sidebar kept from Live-B: rounds with times, email round alerts (on for a registered player), the venue QR with hall TV and print links, and the legend, which now explains the blank result box.
