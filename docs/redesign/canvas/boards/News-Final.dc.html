Notes on this board

  1. 1One pin, with an end date. "Pinned until Sun, Oct 11" is set by the editor and the pin drops off by itself. A partner event's pin says "Registers on the organizer's site ↗", never Register.
  2. 2Category chips with counts, plus From: All · Facebook · LCA. Both live in the address, so a filtered view can be shared.
  3. 3Facebook first. Posts on the LCA Facebook page are pulled in by our server (no Facebook plugin) and listed in Latest with their own text and photo, marked "From our Facebook page ↗". Site stories are marked LCA.
  4. 4A promoted post (part 2, frame C) becomes a site story with a headline and its own page, and keeps "First posted on our Facebook page ↗".
  5. 5"Load 10 more" keeps the footer reachable. Browse by year opens /news/2026, /news/2025 and so on; older years point to the old site.
  6. 6Highlights, not results. Three cards, newest finished LCA event first: headline, one line of section winners, one photo, and Full results, which opens the event's existing results page. No standings or crosstables on News.
  7. 7Each card is written in the event's Recap tab (frame B). Before the director publishes, the card shows the headline and winners from the final standings, and the photo slot falls back to the event name on navy (third card).
  8. 8From the clubs lists posts written on a club page's management side, with the club's colour dot and name. Each post also shows on its club page and in the weekly email.
  9. 9"Post your club's news" speaks to whoever keeps the club page. Assumed here: the region's representative posts for the club (open for K).
  10. 10Share to Facebook. Every story and recap has a share-ready card (image, title, one line) made when it is published, so a link posted on Facebook shows the picture.
  11. 11Email digest, Mondays at 8:00 AM: new stories and recaps, upcoming LCA events and club news. One-click unsubscribe.
  12. 12The Recap tab opens when the event is complete, that is, when the director finalizes standings. Before that it shows greyed with "Opens when standings are final".
  13. 13Filled in from the final standings: a suggested headline and the section winners. Winners are read-only here; a correction made in Pairings & results flows through.
  14. 14The director's part: a few lines (up to 400 characters) and up to four photos, one marked for the card, from a phone or from the LCA Facebook page's recent posts. Photos publish only with the consent box ticked.
  15. 15Publish recap fills the card in Recent tournaments, adds a Results row to Latest and "Read the recap" to the results page, makes the share card and goes in the next email. Share to Facebook is offered straight after.

K's feedback, and where it landed

  1. 1"I like A best."The page is News-A: Latest, the pin with an end date, Load 10 more and the archive (notes 1, 2, 5). A's featured story is gone; Recent tournaments does that job.
  2. 2"We do have results pages... just highlights of recent ones."Recent tournaments: three highlight cards, each with Full results to the existing results page (note 6).
  3. 3"Some sort of admin tab... after it's complete... add the headline."The Recap tab in Workspace, frame B (notes 12 to 15), and on a phone in part 2 (note 18).
  4. 4"The bulk of posting will likely be done from the Facebook."Facebook posts sit in Latest with a source mark (note 3), Promote to story (note 4, and part 2 note 16), share-ready cards (note 10), recap photos from Facebook posts (note 14), and a Follow card in the rail.
  5. 5"I like the latest section of A."Kept as Latest, top of the page (notes 2, 5).
  6. 6"The from the clubs section that C has."Its own section under Recent tournaments, with club colour dots (note 8).
  7. 7"Each club, in their page management, to post their own news."Posted from the club page's management side, with the "Post your club's news" prompt (note 9). Who posts is open for K.

The Recap tab is the tournament's own after-event job, so the director never goes looking for a news editor. The site does the dull part (headline, winners, links, share card) and leaves the human part: a few lines and a photo. Nothing here repeats the results page; the card always points back to it.
