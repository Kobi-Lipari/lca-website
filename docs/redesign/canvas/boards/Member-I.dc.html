Questions

4
Ask us: LouisianaChess@gmail.com

Is this the same as US Chess membership?

No. US Chess membership lets you play rated games anywhere in the country, and you buy it from US Chess. LCA membership is Louisiana's state association: it supports chess here and is required to enter the tournaments LCA runs.

When does my membership end?

One year from the day you join. Renewing early adds a year on top of the time you have left, so it never loses you time. We email you 30 days and 7 days before it ends.

Do I need to be a member to play in LCA events?

For tournaments LCA runs, yes, unless the event says otherwise; club-run events set their own rule. If you aren't a member yet, it's added at checkout when you register.

Who counts as a child on the Family membership?

A K–12 student you're the parent or guardian of. Family covers you and up to 3; a 4th is a Scholastic membership at $5 in the same payment. We never ask for a grade, a birthdate or a child's email.

Are there any fees on top of the price?

[Board to confirm the card fee.] Nothing else. Tournament entry fees are separate and the same for everyone.

Can I get a refund?

[Board to confirm the refund rule.] If a payment went wrong, write to LouisianaChess@gmail.com and we'll put it right.
