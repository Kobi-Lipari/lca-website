3 · Compact table with a side panel

Dense one-line rows on the left and a 380px sticky panel on the right that shows Tourn-A’s rail until a row is picked, then Tourn-B’s preview, so nothing ever expands or pushes the table.

K’s feedback applied: K’s “what is on the right on A is there at first until a tournament is selected”, applied to the table: A’s New to tournaments?, Latest results and For organizers cards at rest, then B’s preview once a row is picked. The bell has a deliberate home in the row gutter, and its options sit inline in the panel with a week and a day ticked. No saved views, no subscribe, no batch actions. Partner panels never show Register or a count.

Best for
Desktop power users and directors who compare many events: dense rows, the full facts one click away, and the table never jumps.
Trade-off
It needs about 1280px; below that the panel becomes a drawer over the table. Text is smaller (13px) and the rows are plain, so it reads least like A. The preview is the same component as the List view’s, so the two views feel alike on wide screens.
Other widths
1280px and up: table plus sticky panel. 1024–1279px: a 400px drawer (states strip). 768–1023px: the bell gutter is always visible. On phones a picked row opens Tourn-B’s full-screen sheet.
