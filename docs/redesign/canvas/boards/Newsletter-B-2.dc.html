Thank you, Sulphur

To every volunteer who ran the State Championship weekend in Sulphur: thank you. 64 players, six sections, five rounds and an annual meeting.

Every round started on time. That was the volunteers' doing.

The new year opens with the Ken Ferguson Memorial in New Orleans. Two board seats are open, Central Louisiana and Southwest Louisiana: to help your region's clubs, reply to this email.

One ask: renew your membership, and bring one new player to a club night.
