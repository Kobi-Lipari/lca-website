Map first, list drawer

The map gets the full width and the list floats in a drawer on its left. Selecting an event turns the drawer into B’s preview; Back returns to the list (K’s “the right side shows at first until a tournament is selected” idea, applied to the map).

Frame 1440 · signed out · two states

Best for

People who think “where” first and scan the whole Gulf South, for example a strong player willing to drive to Houston or Jackson, and anyone who likes a big map.

Trade-off

The drawer covers a third of the map and shows about nine rows at a time. The “in view” count can differ from the toolbar count, so the wording has to stay clear. While an event is selected the list is hidden behind Back.

Feedback applied

K’s own pattern from the list (one thing until an event is selected, then B’s preview) reused in the drawer; the bell replaces the alert card and the subscribe idea, with its signed-out state drawn; the decided toolbar; no drive times or location button; Lafayette Open shown as a partner.

Other widths and phones

Between 1024 and 1279px the drawer narrows to 340px. On tablets the drawer becomes a bottom sheet. On phones: “full-screen map with a bottom card”. Signed out here because the popover shows the signed-out state.
