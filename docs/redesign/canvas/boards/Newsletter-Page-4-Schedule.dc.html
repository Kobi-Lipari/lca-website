A phone had no Send before; it now sits at the foot of What members will get.

For the build

  • The time is Central time, shown in words.
  • A day or time already past cannot be chosen.
  • The people are counted again when it sends: a member who joins before then gets it, and one who has unsubscribed does not.
  • The letter can be changed until it sends; what goes out is the letter as it stands then.
  • Changing the time, Send now and “Do not send it” are open to everyone who may send the newsletter.
  • “Do not send it” puts the letter back as a draft and deletes nothing.
  • Each of these is written in the activity log with who did it.
  • Only one newsletter can be scheduled or sending at a time.
