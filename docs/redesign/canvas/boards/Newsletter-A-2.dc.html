Dear members,

Thank you to every volunteer who ran the State Championship weekend in Sulphur, and to everyone who stayed to put the boards away.

The new year starts busy: the Ken Ferguson Memorial in January, a youth meet every month through May, and two board seats we would like to fill.

One ask. If your membership runs out this winter, please renew, and bring one new player to a club night. That is how every club below began.

See you over the board,
LCA President
