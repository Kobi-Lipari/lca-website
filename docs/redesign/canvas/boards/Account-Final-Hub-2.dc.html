G

Events: who makes them, when they list, who charges, rating reports

K, Mon, Oct 19, and page 14. The insets are drawn at 85%.

5
1

Who makes an event and names its director. Standing directors make their own events here, any time. The president can always make one and name its one-event director. LCA admins and regional representatives can draft one and name its one-event director (Mon, Oct 19). Later, every member, with all the same tools but unlisted, shared by link (page 14).

2

When it lists. A standing director’s event lists on Tournaments the moment it is published. LCA admins get a note to look it over afterwards; nothing waits for them (Mon, Oct 19).

3

Who charges a fee above $0, for now. LCA admins, club reps, the president and standing directors. Anyone else who types a price above $0 is told why, in plain words (G1), and can still run the event at $0. A standing director is told how the money is held and reached (G2).

4

Rating reports. Every event gets the Rating report stage, the last of the five on its card (Account-L).

$10
Entry fees need a payout account first

LCA takes entry fees through its own Stripe account. Before your event can charge a fee, LCA sets up an account for you inside LCA’s Stripe account, called a connected account (Stripe Connect). It keeps your event’s money apart from LCA’s and pays it out to you. Until then, your event can run at $0.
