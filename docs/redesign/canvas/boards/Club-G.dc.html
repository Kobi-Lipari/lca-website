About Baton Rouge Chess Club

Sample data: the club's words

We've been meeting in Baton Rouge since 2009. Most Saturdays you'll find fifteen to twenty of us at the Jones Creek library playing long, slow games, with a few boards of blitz in the corner for anyone waiting.

Wednesdays are the opposite: casual games and coffee-shop chess at Boba ParTea, where nobody keeps score and people wander in from 6:00 PM until the shop closes. Beginners sit down with 2000-rated players every week, and nobody minds.

Come once and you'll know whether it's your kind of club.

Cost

Free. Saturday rated games are $5 toward the room.

Find us online

Facebook: Baton Rouge Chess Club ↗

Website: this page is our website

Who is welcome5no member count or roster

All ages and all levels. Kids under 12 come with a parent. You don't need an LCA or US Chess membership for a club night. Bring a board and clock if you have them, and ask for Marcus at the front table.

When and where Baton Rouge Chess Club meets

In the club's words

Saturdays from 10:00 AM at Jones Creek Branch Library: long games, 90 minutes each plus 30 seconds a move, usually done by 2:00 PM. Wednesdays from 6:00 PM at Boba ParTea on O'Neil Lane: casual games, no clock needed, until the shop closes. We skip holiday weekends. Check our Facebook page before your first visit in case a Saturday moves.
