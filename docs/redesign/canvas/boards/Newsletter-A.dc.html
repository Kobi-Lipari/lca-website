Notes on this board

  1. 1The sheet is the email. One white column, the email's own width and type: what the leaders type is exactly what arrives. The grey margin lines (handles, who edited, History) are never sent.
  2. 2One person in a section at a time. "President is editing" locks that section for the others until they leave it. Every section keeps its History, with Restore (B5).
  3. 3An empty section is left out. Nothing to say about the board this month? Leave it empty and its heading does not go out.
  4. 4The send rule, open for K: someone must mark the issue ready. The editors get a reminder the day before; if no one marks it, it waits and nothing is sent.
  5. 5A report without tracking. Sent, delivered, bounced, unsubscribed, spam reports. LCA does not track who opens an email or what they click (B2).

Note for the canvas

What: A letter from LCA to its members, and the page where it is made is that letter, open on the screen: the president's note, then five short sections, mostly words and one photo at most. The least design and the most voice of the four; it gives up pictures, layout and anything that fills itself in, so an issue is only as good as what someone writes that month.
