Notes on this board

  2. 2A masthead with a name. "Louisiana Chess Bulletin" is a working name, open for K; "LCA Newsletter" is the plain fallback. The volume line counts itself.

Lead story

A note from the president

Thank you, Sulphur

Sixty-four players, three days, and a weekend that ran on time. A word on the year ahead, and one thing we ask.

Photo described: rows of players at their boards during a round in the tournament hall.

Thank you to every volunteer who ran the State Championship weekend in Sulphur: the directors, the people at the door, and everyone who stayed to fold the tables.

The new year starts busy. The Ken Ferguson Memorial comes back to New Orleans in January, the youth season runs to its Grand Prix Final in May, and two board seats wait for the right people.

One ask for December: renew if your membership runs out this winter, and bring one new player to a club night.

LCA President
