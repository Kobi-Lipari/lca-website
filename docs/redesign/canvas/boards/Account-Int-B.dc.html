Trade-off

Two rails on the site that look alike (the My LCA rail and the admin sidebar), which is what K wanted to get away from. The rail costs 240px that the console and the checklist cannot spare at 1024px, so they fold it, and the same-shell promise weakens exactly where Chris works hardest. A person-centred home and task-centred screens share one URL space, so an admin backstopping an event lands under My something that is not theirs. Every decided route moves. The single-role member gets no rail, so the area looks different for different people. In return the volunteer with five roles never scrolls a long page, and each role’s work has a stable URL.
