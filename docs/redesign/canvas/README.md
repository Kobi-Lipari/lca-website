# The canvas, as published

`canvas.json` is the layout of the "LCA Look & Feel Options" canvas: every board's page, position, size and title, every note, and the page order.

`boards/` holds the boards that exist nowhere else in this repository: the round 2 and round 3 options for the clubs directory and the club page, the decided boards for tournament day, scholastic, news and about, the tournaments option boards, and the round 2 options for membership and giving, My LCA, workspace and admin, and the scanner. The first-round option boards are in `../lca-redesign-boards/boards` and the earlier decided boards in `../decided-boards`; together the three folders are the canvas.

Each board is one self-contained `.dc.html` file in the kit's style (`../lca-redesign-boards/kit.css`).
